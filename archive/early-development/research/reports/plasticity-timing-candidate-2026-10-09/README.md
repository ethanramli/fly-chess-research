## Finding and scope

Handler et al. (2019), Figure S3D and accompanying results, report bidirectional KC-MBON modulation in gamma2 with forward versus backward KC/DAN pairing. The article's main mechanistic receptor perturbation evidence is in gamma4. Timing-dependent direction therefore has direct gamma2 physiological support, while transferring the complete receptor model to these released cells remains an approximation. Sources: [primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9012144/), [anatomical nomenclature](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9984194/). Correct compartment spelling is gamma2-alpha-prime1 for MBON12/PPL103, not gamma2-alpha1. Search extract coverage only; no new visual figure or full supplement audit is claimed. Raw responses: [Handler](handler-source.json), [anatomy](anatomy-source.json).

This is an alternative to baseline-dip plasticity, not evidence that the existing dynamics produce a dip. The old static audit always emits missing signed-baseline trace, coordinates/receptors and stabilization blockers. Those legitimately prevent certifying its specified branch; they do not rule out every biologically motivated bidirectional model. Exact receptor identity at each synapse is unavailable, but a declared compartment-level physiological approximation can be investigated without pretending those identities were measured. Stabilization remains a separate coexistence question; requiring its solution before testing directional plasticity reverses the intended staged order. Frozen v4 and its refusal are preserved.

## Mechanism to build next

Use an explicitly phenomenological timing-dependent local rule restricted to the justified candidate, with separate finite KC and DAN traces. Local causal order supplies direction; the existing bounded gain kernel can supply a numerical integrator, but it is not the paper's biochemical equation. KC activity followed by DAN activity supplies the depression branch; preceding DAN activity followed by KC activity supplies the potentiation branch. The overlap/zero-lag case must follow the measured depression regime rather than cancel by construction. A single decaying root tag evaluated only at the endpoint cannot distinguish these orders and must not be relabeled receptor dynamics.

Both traces are generated from neural activity, saved in checkpoints and cleared or decayed according to explicit episode semantics. No chess label or target gain enters the plasticity function. Do not silently map simulation steps to experimental seconds: the reported gamma2 timing (forward 0 s, backward -1.2 s) constrains order, but current dynamics lack a biological clock calibration. Numerical timescales must be labeled engineering choices and tested for robustness, not fitted to puzzle answers.

For a controlled future teaching condition, verified adverse feedback can pair cue with a PPL103 pulse; verified favorable feedback can present the pulse before a replay of the same cue. This is an engineered temporal teaching code, **not** a natural reward pathway or discovered reward-omission response. It must be declared separately, with identical pulse/cue doses and schedule-matched controls. The teacher chooses stimulus order only; it never computes parameter deltas. Whether such a replay teaches the original choice, interferes with other choices, or moves the fixed readout correctly is unproved.

Before graph integration: verify the actual encoder reaches these 120 KCs, quantify candidate-level eligible-trace separation from saved evidence if available, and specify the timing rule against measured order/overlap constraints. Do not enlarge the mask or change readout coefficients to force a pass. Then test numerical direction, reversible bounds, temporal reset and interference. Live association/coexistence/reversal and chess transfer remain essential and require authorization under the user's no-training instruction. A timing candidate that only remembers isolated cues has not solved plasticity for chess.
