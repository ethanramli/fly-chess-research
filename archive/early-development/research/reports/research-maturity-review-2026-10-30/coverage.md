# Review coverage and limitations

2026-10-30. The review is complete as a bounded advisory assessment, not an exhaustive proof that no other problem exists.

## What received substantive inspection

- Project instructions, contribution/feedback goals, current status, relevant decision/journal history, active r15 protocol/registration and corrective handoffs.
- Current encoder -> factory/receiver -> sparse dynamics -> local memory -> rehearsal -> evaluation/gates paths and their relevant inherited implementations.
- Generator/split/certificate semantics, training inputs, safe aggregate separation output, audit/source-pinning/resume/supervisor paths and selected historical reports listed in [source-map.md](source-map.md).
- Literature register, novelty scoping note, prior upstream analysis, official current venue rules and selected primary studies/reviews.

Some long outputs were truncated. Only visible portions count as read; decisive findings were checked through smaller reads or direct reproductions. No claim is made that every source line or historical run was reviewed.

The review directory itself grew after inventory/indexing. Counts are snapshots and do not measure substantive review coverage.

## Explicitly not performed

- No graph training, new full neural replay, independent cold-start reproduction, live-system stress/recovery run or energy benchmark.
- No sealed validation/final row access or new classifier evaluation on those sets. The all-split counts check was performed under a separate recorded authorization by another session; this review read its output and code, without repeating it.
- No original wet-lab dataset reanalysis, complete supplement/figure audit or exhaustive systematic literature search.
- No live upstream checkpoint use, pretrained-weight import, active run restart, experiment-control command or frozen-code/gate change.
- No interview/knowledge assessment, target-year eligibility decision, actual submission-author review or future SRC/IRB determination.
- No award, peer-review selection, acceptance-probability or fixed numeric deduction prediction.

## Evidence independence

The new training comparator rechecks certificates with the production checker: it is independent of neural learning, not an independent chess-rules implementation. Current run results are reported from preserved project artifacts; this review did not rerun the published full-r14 audit. Primary-paper statements retain the access limitations in external-sources.md. Old findings repaired during the review are reconciled in the main report instead of silently retained as current defects.

## Integrity and remaining work

Source, data, runs, frozen protocols and sealed contents were left unchanged by this review. New scripts/results are confined to this report; dated navigation/journal/decision/status entries only point to the assessment and its limits. Current r15 audit state was a point-in-time observation, not a terminal claim.

Unresolved scientific questions, future confirmation and reproduction are the reviewed research's next work. They are not unfinished steps of this advisory review and are not automatically authorized experiments.
