# Full-graph forcing-shortlist comparison

This read-only paired test used the pinned full connectome, the original-input naive memory and the smothered-mate development fixture. The fixture's independent audit covers all 32 legal actions and identifies `c4g8` as its sole successful move. Each condition was assessed twice in balanced all-legal/shortlist/shortlist/all-legal order under the same frozen memory and 50% presentation pacing. The shortlist selected 16 moves with four quiet slots; candidate generation took 30.7 ms.

The all-legal assessments averaged **22.08 seconds** for 32 candidate presentations. The forcing shortlist averaged **12.47 seconds** for 16 presentations, a **43.5% lower assessment time** on this one position. Choices agreed across both repetitions: the fly preferred `c4b4` under either candidate set. Independent verification says `c4b4` fails the mate task. The shortlist retained `c4g8`, the sole independently certified successful move, but the fly did not choose it. Its MBON preference was 0.013221 versus 0.020617 for `c4b4`, a 0.007396 score gap. So the remaining error is neural ranking, not candidate omission on this fixture.

This demonstrates a promising one-position compute reduction with no move omission of the known successful action. It also demonstrates that candidate filtering did not repair the neural ranking error. It is not MCTS integration, a training result, learning-per-experience evidence, or transfer. The root puzzle has no predecessor board, so its changed-square delta was empty; check/capture/new-attack features were still computed from the root. The shortlist remains experimental until paired search tests cover multiple training and counterfactual/held-out positions, verify consequences, and compare choice quality under matched compute. Do not call geometric attack tags proof of a sound threat.

The raw `report.json` records all legal and shortlist scores, tags, omitted actions, proof hashes, input/source identities, repeated timings and resources. `COMPLETE` binds the published report. Peak RSS was 109.80 MiB, process CPU time 36.69 seconds, and free system memory at completion 1,393.51 MiB.
