## Repository-specific corrections before acting

1. **The constant encoder is a numerical test double.** Real implementations already exist in `chess-cue.mjs`, compartment/relational variants and public frame cues. For example, `createChessCueEncoder` projects piece/color/square, action and declared task/history tokens into identified eligible KCs with collision/drop accounting. That is not proof of relational sufficiency, but replacing a supposed production constant encoder would be the wrong diagnosis. Inspect and assess these implementations before designing another.
2. **The proposed receiver study overlaps existing G0 work.** The seed-3 registration already covers acquisition/interference/retained-versus-reset reversal, informative/absent/disabled and three shuffled arms. Its required all-three-seed comparison remains the prerequisite for choosing an intervention. Reconcile/audit that lineage when the researcher resumes; identify what a new common-exposure/sentinel assay adds. Do not discard or repeat frozen experiments because an external reviewer lacked their detail. A diagnostic replication cannot itself qualify original G0.
3. **The active temporal receiver differs from the reviewed endpoint rule.** The replication runner constructs `RateTimingMemory`; temporal sessions require `observeTrajectory`. The isolated Stockfish fixture uses `ReversalMemory.observe`. Correct conclusions about the latter cannot be applied as if they characterized the former or supplied a ready interchangeable receiver.
4. **The scalar-instability discussion needs its guard.** For the endpoint rule with fixed activity, its multiplier is `1 + eta*dt*D`. The code rejects `eta*dt*(-D)>1`, so accepted fixed-activity updates have a multiplier in[0,1]. The response's generic possibility of oscillation/instability is not established for this guarded scalar subcase. Network recurrence may still create more complicated behavior; no graph cause is diagnosed here.

These corrections refine an otherwise actionable review. They do not invalidate its main concerns about the uncalibrated proxy, update alignment, terminal skipping or incomplete causal controls.
