**Prepared:** 2026-09-28  
**Status:** Filtered for researchers whose institutions provide English-language professional profiles and whose research communication is conducted in English. Verify current details before contacting anyone.

## Important language caveat

“English-speaking” is difficult to verify from a web profile. This list therefore uses a practical standard: the researcher works at an institution where English is an official or primary academic working language, maintains an English-language lab/profile page, and publishes in English. This is evidence that an English-language professional inquiry is appropriate; it is **not** a claim about native fluency or preferred spoken language.

Before requesting a video meeting, ask whether English is convenient and offer email-based feedback as an alternative.

## Shared profile

The core contacts are relevant because their work combines several of the following:

- neural circuits and behavior;
- learning, memory, value, or plasticity;
- connectomics or circuit reconstruction;
- computational, quantitative, or machine-learning methods;
- mechanistic rather than benchmark-only explanations;
- experience working in international research environments.

No one listed here has agreed to mentor the project. They may be unavailable, may require university enrollment, or may refer the request to a trainee or colleague.

## English-language outreach template

**Subject:** Brief guidance on connectome-constrained cognitive modeling

> Dear Professor [Surname],
>
> I am an independent researcher developing a computational cognitive-science project asking whether a model constrained by a Drosophila connectome can learn structured chess-position evaluation and generalize to unfamiliar positions. Chess is only the testbed; I am not claiming that biological flies understand chess.
>
> I read your work on [specific paper, circuit, or method], especially [one concrete detail]. It seems relevant to my question about [learning, value, circuit computation, connectome modeling, or generalization].
>
> I have drafted a protocol with held-out tests and ablations separating the constrained model, training signal, external search, and interface. I am seeking brief methodological guidance or a referral to a suitable mentor—not asking you to take responsibility for the project. If English is convenient, would you be open to a short email exchange or a brief call? Email-only feedback is also completely fine.
>
> I can send a one-page summary and protocol if useful. I understand if your schedule or institutional policy prevents you from responding.
>
> Best regards,  
> [Full name]  
> [School or independent-research status]  
> [Location/time zone]  
> [Project link]
