# Idea bank

Capture curiosities without committing to them. An idea is a starting point, not yet a research gap.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Prompts

- What problem have I seen people struggle with?
- What claim or explanation am I unsure about?
- What population, place, time, or context might make a broad topic specific?
- What evidence could answer the question, and can I access it ethically?
