# Board/action cue association experiment

2026-10-05. Frozen candidate engineering experiment, not verified chess feedback or biological validation.

The [naive input panel](board-action-cues.md) established distinguishable board/action signals without learning. The earlier [GF diagnostic](../diagnostics/gf-readout-probe.md) reused synthetic association cues and an unclipped readout. This follow-up tests new encoder seeds (10111, 10133, 10151, 10177, 10211, 10243), the actual public board/action encoder, and the previously frozen **clipped** input-domain calibration. It does not fit an offset, gain, polarity or learning rate to the new results.

The two cue descriptions are `Qa2` and `Qh7` in the engineering fixture. They share board/context features and some stimulated KCs; unlike the original disjoint synthetic cues, overlap is intentional and independently audited. The six seeds vary the fixed hash projection, not the chess family. The same naive calibration is retained across seeds and learning phases; out-of-panel saturation is recorded as a potential failure, not hidden by recalibration.

**Experimental punishment contingencies are not chess consequences.** Initial punished identity alternates by seed index and reverses after acquisition. This deliberately sometimes punishes the mating action because the task is controlled cue association/reversal, not teaching chess truth. No verifier is run or consumed, no chess reward is claimed, and these memories must not be promoted as a trained chess checkpoint. The declared mate horizon is visible cue/task context only. The teacher supplies balanced presentations and optional direct pulses to the mapped PPL103 cells. It never supplies parameter changes; local measured KC/DAN responses determine permitted efficacy updates.

Four conditions per seed: informative contingency, absent external stimulation, phase-wise pulse-count-matched shuffled stimulation, and informative stimulation with plasticity disabled. Four acquisition and twelve reversal presentations yield 384 bounded events. No plasticity occurs during baseline, acquisition, persistence, reversal, reset or restoration probes. Choice reads only two GF rates, through fixed min/max calibration and clipped max aggregation, favoring the lower score, with exact ties choosing A. MBON values and raw/signed GF signals are retained for diagnosis; they cannot replace the frozen choice decoder after results appear.

The [protocol](../../../research/data/chess-cue-association-v1/protocol.json) requires correct acquired and reversed choices for every informative seed, positive target-signed effects, reversal improvement above absent/shuffled controls at one-sided sign p<0.05, exact stored-state persistence/reset/restoration, disabled invariance, matched pulse counts, and fixed graph/nonplastic hashes. Every computational gate must pass. Neither more favorable rate changes nor a passing consistency audit can override a failed choice. The original gamma4 failures and old resting-reference clipping failure remain preserved.

Completed result: [raw report, independent audit and diagnostics](../../../research/reports/chess-cue-association-v1-2026-10-05/README.md). Overall acceptance fails: acquired and reversed GF choices are correct in only 3/6, and no informative seed changes its choice from baseline in either phase. All 384 updates audit correctly; persistence/reset/restoration, controls and fixed state pass. Five reversal effects exceed controls but do not achieve the required choices. Removing clipping diagnostically still yields 3/6 in each phase, while MBON diagnostics yield 6/6 acquisition and 5/6 reversal; neither can replace the frozen decoder/gates. Common-cell interference and readout preference need causal investigation. This result prevents treating the new cue/calibration interface as a working learner.

Later diagnostic: [60 memory-subset interventions](../diagnostics/memory-ablation.md) retain only unique, shared or other cells' efficacy changes, alongside intact and naive controls. None changes a decoded choice; all remain 3/6 in each phase. Resetting shared state after training does not rescue behavior, but does not rule out interference while learning or nonlinear interactions. Every mask audits correctly and intact/naive numerical responses reproduce exactly. No new learning rule or acceptance was substituted.
