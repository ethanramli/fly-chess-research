## Primary evidence and limits

[McCurdy et al., Nature Communications 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21388-w), accessed 2026-10-06, identifies reward-encoding PAM-β′2a activity and depression of KC-to-avoidance-MBON-γ5β′2a synapses during punishment-omission reversal. [Yamada et al., eLife 2023](https://elifesciences.org/articles/79042), particularly [its figures](https://elifesciences.org/articles/79042/figures), lists PAM02 among reward DANs that can induce appetitive memory under optogenetic activation, while distinguishing shorter-lasting combinatorial PAM-γ5/β′2a memory from PAM-α1 long-term memory. The latter system is not implemented here. These studies motivate a separately tested experimental appetitive stimulation condition, not a natural chess reward pathway or arbitrarily long persistence.

The existing pinned coarse mapping has six PAM02 neurons per side and 84 qualifying KC-to-MBON01 edges, separate from PPL103/MBON12's 786 edges. These unequal masks and existing wiring must remain fixed. Type-level correspondence is an approximation; it does not supply synapse-coordinate compartment or receptor identity.
