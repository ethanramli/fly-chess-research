# Reversal mechanism: source evidence and implementation limits

2026-10-05. Status: investigated candidate; no new learning rule implemented. Preserve original gamma4 V1/V2 failures and their equations.

Later implementation addendum, 2026-10-05: retrieved upstream model code at commit `1610c80072fe8bb59bd397e7a61f716393a509b9`, preserved its GPL license in the inspection cache, and implemented an isolated computational comparison in `reversal-memory.mjs`. Its numerical/restore tests pass; no full-graph candidate learning experiment or chess integration has run. The original status above describes the earlier evidence check.

Pinned primary implementation: [models_base.py](https://github.com/InsectRobotics/IncentiveCircuit/blob/1610c80072fe8bb59bd397e7a61f716393a509b9/src/incentive/models_base.py), particularly `update_weights` and `dopaminergic`, and [circuit.py](https://github.com/InsectRobotics/IncentiveCircuit/blob/1610c80072fe8bb59bd397e7a61f716393a509b9/src/incentive/circuit.py). The rule is `w_next = w + eta * D * (k + w - w_rest)`. The model separately maps nonnegative DAN activity through signed modulatory coefficients. Negative D is a modulatory polarity, not a negative reward or a negative DAN firing rate. Recovery of an inactive KC's depressed efficacy requires nonzero appropriate DAN modulation; absence of all DAN modulation leaves efficacy unchanged.

Our comparison applies this form to dimensionless local gains with resting gain 1, bounded at 0.2–1.8. Eta defaults to 0.5, reflecting the upstream three-step default's `1/(steps-1)` rate, not measured fly kinetics. Measured excess KC/DAN endpoint rates are normalized by an engineering scale of 1.5; within each mapped group, D is the negative mean normalized excess activity of identified DANs. A one-step Euler observation is permitted only when its recovery coefficient is at most 1. No timing eligibility or receptor kinetics are added. This is a new approximation of a published computational rule, not replication of the complete upstream model. Its dense circuitry, biases, hand-selected magnitudes, trial history arrays and reinforcement input matrix are not imported. Existing graph/signs/nonplastic parameters are preserved. Only selected positive-edge efficacy gains persist; checkpoints include exact gain arrays, event count and mapping/config/base identity.

[Candidate mapping](../../../archive/early-development/research/reports/reversal-candidate-2026-10-05/mapping.json) identifies 870 existing edges: MBON12 groups contain 452 left-KC and 334 right-KC inputs, and MBON01 beta-prime groups contain 76 left-KC and 8 right-KC inputs. One PPL103 per side and six PAM02 per side are mapped. Gamma inputs to MBON01 are excluded from PAM02 beta-prime modulation. Eligibility uses actual `KCa'b'-` and `KCg-` annotation types, existing edges and KC hemisphere; it remains a coarse subtype-level approximation without synapse coordinates/receptor identity. The first naming check incorrectly expected `KCap-`; [its incomplete inventory](../../../archive/early-development/research/reports/reversal-candidate-2026-10-05/mapping-inventory.json) is preserved, and a mapping test now covers the released notation. No absent edges were invented to repair the mask.

This class is a candidate comparison, not the completed biological feedback receiver. Full-graph association, matched controls, reliable reversal and independent biological predictions remain untested for it. It must not enable chess learning on the strength of numerical unit tests.

Subsequent experimental addendum: the [frozen exposure-controlled comparison](recovery-comparison.md) now passes its complete computational gate on six new seeds, including correct reversed choices in 6/6, matched absent/shuffled/disabled controls, causal memory checks and independent audit of all 384 local updates. This supersedes the preceding statement that its association comparison was unrun. It does not supersede the missing biological validation, natural interfaces, GF behavior, full-model training or chess transfer requirements.

The project needs verified consequences to change persistent local state and improve later decisions, including changed positions where a superficially similar tactic fails. The [observed diagnostic](../../../archive/early-development/research/reports/reversal-diagnostic-2026-10-05/README.md) shows persistent old preference in failed gamma4 reversals. It does not uniquely identify missing dynamics.

[McCurdy et al., Nature Communications 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21388-w) experimentally connect punishment omission, increased MBON-gamma2alpha'1 responses, PAM-beta'2a activation and reduced MBON-gamma5beta'2a responses. This supports investigating a circuit relay rather than externally inserting a negative reward update. The source's qualitative account is not a fitted synapse equation for this rate model. Accessed 2026-10-05 via indexed primary-source content; direct full-page requests were blocked. Figure 5 and the abstract informed this check; full methods and source-data extraction remain incomplete.

[Gkanias et al., eLife 2022](https://elifesciences.org/articles/75611.pdf) propose a dopamine-dependent rule that distinguishes active/inactive KCs and includes recovery toward resting strength. It is a computational hypothesis, not established receptor kinetics. Its described recovery can act when a KC is inactive during dopamine signaling: **reversal while another cue is punished differs from merely omitting feedback on the old cue**. This motivates logging both cue activity and DAN responses throughout each trial, including the other cue. It does not justify applying a generic recovery term on every unrewarded trial. Indexed primary-source text was accessible; full PDF requests were blocked. No complete equation has been transcribed or imported.

Pinned local ontology aliases identify MBON12, PPL103, PAM02 and MBON01 with the candidate cell types. The [anatomy report](../../../archive/early-development/research/reports/instinct-design-2026-10-05/anatomy-with-relay.json) supplies exact roots and existing signed edges. Its type-level KC inputs do not label synapse compartments or receptor identity. Choosing every KC input to an MBON would therefore be a declared cell-level approximation, not an exact compartment mask.

[Frozen functional probe](../../../archive/early-development/research/reports/relay-probe-2026-10-05/README.md): two directly connected PAM02 receivers increase following MBON12 stimulation in the unchanged full graph; other receivers have mixed effects. GF changes are small. These measurements support propagation in the present kernel but not omission encoding, learning, behavior or chess transfer.

Unfinished requirements before a new learning experiment:

1. Recover complete primary methods, supplementary data and the published model equations/code with version provenance. Separate observed effects from hand-selected computational constants.
2. Define an auditable local eligibility mask. If only a coarse cell-level mask is available, state which gamma/alpha'/beta' components are unresolved and independently test that approximation.
3. Specify persistent efficacy, transient activity/eligibility/receptor state, numerical integration, permitted changes and atomic checkpoint fields. No teacher-calculated deltas and no changed graph signs.
4. Specify how simulated DAN activity becomes compartment-local modulation. An ordinary signed DAN connection and a dopamine plasticity signal are distinct; avoid double-counting them.
5. Freeze development-seed association, exposure-controlled reversal, absent/shuffled/disabled controls, local-state observations and independent timing/omission predictions. Keep V1/V2 gates and records intact.
6. Require reliable changed choice and causal memory checks before coupling to puzzle feedback. Check candidate-specific GF effects separately; a measurable rate difference alone cannot enable the proposed GF choice policy.

This card deliberately leaves equations unspecified where their source or mapping is unverified. It does not authorize a generic negative update, manually driven omission reward, or natural-sensory claims. The next executable mechanism must satisfy these requirements; chess learning remains the goal.
