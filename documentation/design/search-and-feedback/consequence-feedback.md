Implemented diagnostics: generated-but-unvisited alternatives that independently satisfy the objective; omitted legal candidates when reviewed and verified; an avoidable bounded-objective miss only when an independently verified alternative succeeds; search rescuing an initially preferred move that misses the objective; observed cutoffs without invented line attribution. ## Replayable consequence certificates: later 2026-10-05 addendum

Current known mate consequences require an AND/OR certificate and a separate replay checker. Attacker success needs one winning move; defender success requires all legal defenses to succeed. Attacker failure requires every candidate to fail within the horizon; defender failure needs one verified escape. Draw and exhausted-horizon leaves fail only this bounded mate objective, not the whole game. The checker binds position, complete PGN history, actor and horizon, reconstructs legal moves, rejects missing/duplicate branches, and validates every required leaf. It imports no search, proof-construction or brain evaluator code; both tools still use the same chess.js rules library.

Defaults remain four reviewed candidates, 4,096 construction nodes total and 1,024 per candidate. Replay has a separate per-candidate node cap equal to its construction allowance; report construction and checking counts separately. Each construction and checking stage also has a 2,000 ms soft deadline (`--proof-ms`); legal-move generation already running cannot be interrupted. Exhaustion of either stage leaves the consequence unknown, with no teaching event. Time limits can affect reproducibility on a loaded computer. Atomic round-state v3 pins the additional sources and time settings; old checkpoints are rejected rather than reinterpreted. Certificate hashes tie each teaching record to its evidence; they do not assign neural credit.
