# Verified contrastive choice feedback

The adaptive puzzle loop previously verified and replayed only the committed move. If every adaptive choice failed, its experience stream contained only failures; a shuffled channel control then had no positive/negative variation to shuffle. A useful higher-information teacher record should also verify at least one legal alternative after commitment and preserve both consequences in the same board, actor, history and task context.

The new teacher-only helper [contrastive-choice-review.mjs](../../../.codebase/src/search-and-feedback/contrastive-choice-review.mjs) accepts a committed move and a small bounded list of candidate alternatives. It independently verifies each action with the existing certificate path. It marks a contrast only when the chosen action is a verified failure and an alternative is a verified success under the declared bounded objective. Proof exhaustion remains unknown and is excluded from `experiences`. The helper does not expose labels during policy choice, compute a neural update, change plasticity, or claim full-game loss from a bounded-mate failure.

This is feedback preparation, not a training experiment. A future runner must freeze how alternatives are proposed without answer leakage, feedback order, retry/stop rules, per-condition experience matching, and budgets before inference. It must replay each known action/context through the existing receiver, send identical experience sequences to absent, nondegenerate shuffled and disabled controls, and measure pre/post all-legal choices plus counterfactual consequence sensitivity. Unknowns generate no event. Persistent state and pending receipts remain subject to the existing atomic checkpoint/audit contract.

The action contrast gives a verified outcome relation but does not explain why the policy preferred the committed move, establish calibrated material evaluation, or assign credit to earlier moves in a delayed line. The neural receiver must independently recheck each certificate. No eligibility across the Monte Carlo search is implied.

The new software tests cover same-context failure/success pairing, successful committed choices, unknown handling, board immutability, and bounded alternative validation. These tests establish plumbing only. Useful persistent puzzle learning and unfamiliar/counterfactual transfer remain unmet.
