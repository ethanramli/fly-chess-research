## Question and estimands

Recommended focused question: **Does a fly-connectome-derived representation, coupled to explicitly engineered experience memory, enable consequence-sensitive transfer to unfamiliar chess positions beyond equally trained retrieval and non-connectome controls?** This is an AI-generated recommendation for researcher adoption. The larger goal remains applying separately learned concepts to unfamiliar problems. One learned safe-capture relation is prerequisite evidence, not that goal achieved.

Separate three estimands: (A) informative experience versus absent/shuffled/frozen learning, on held-out consequences; (B) released topology versus matched alternative representations under the identical memory/controller; (C) all separately learned concepts versus every single-concept and fresh/shuffled comparator on untaught combinations. A passing A does not imply B or C. Primary choices are unaided; search-assisted choices are a separate condition with identical node/evaluation/depth/time budgets.

## Transfer mechanism: the decision that must precede a reasoning run

In the inspected exact-key implementation, `query(z)` returns zero/unknown when z is absent. With known-key precedence, the hybrid score on such a miss is only the neural fallback. Thus changing stored values at other keys cannot affect that miss, conditional on the same neural state. Exact storage has no cross-key transfer operation. If distinct boards share a key, that is representation aliasing, which can help or confound; it is not automatically conceptual transfer. Audit exact hits, collisions and nearest training distance separately.

Three honest alternatives:

1. Keep exact memory and seek transfer solely in independently acquired neural state. This preserves the current controller but still requires a working transferable neural learner; the store has not supplied that mechanism.
2. **Recommended first engineered candidate:** a bounded nearest-neighbour consequence readout over frozen graph features, paired with the identical rule on raw-eye and random/rewired features. This makes cross-cue influence explicit without biological-memory repair. It tests representation-mediated generalization; similarity transfer alone cannot pass the later combination claim.
3. Engineer a relational/compositional controller. Potentially closer to the broad goal, but any chess relations, concept routing and composition rules in that controller become supplied intelligence and need controller-only ablations. Do not begin here without a falsifiable unmet requirement for option 2.

Concrete candidate for option 2, **all new choices proposed, not literature-derived gates**: use the 5,177 KC endpoint activity vector as the primary graph descriptor (five MBON means as a predeclared secondary representation, not a selected winner). Store at most 128 candidate episodes initially; use float32 copied vectors and exhaustive bounded distance computation, no new index dependency. Candidate moves are embodied in after-board/marks, not pooled by move UCI across unrelated boards. Scale by a fixed training-only unsupervised per-feature RMS with a declared epsilon; use Euclidean distance and the mean of the 3 nearest episode consequences. At fewer than 3 entries use those available; empty store returns unknown/zero. Preserve exact signed latest evidence for duplicate descriptors; least-recently-updated eviction; no max-ever-return rule. Resolve history-visible aliases before use. Pin numeric epsilon, duplicate tolerance, ties, capacity and normalization hashes in the eventual registration. Capacity 128 and k=3 are modest engineering starting choices, not estimated optima or sufficient chess capacity. A development-only sensitivity table at k=1/3/5 and capacity 32/128 can reject fragility; choose once using per-criterion validation and count every attempted configuration. No final-data selection.

This departs from the current sparse exact-key schema; make a separate component/version rather than changing v1. Graph and comparator descriptors may differ in dimension: pair both a native-dimension information control and a common-dimension fixed random projection (proposed 64 dimensions), with projection seed frozen and no labels. Report both; a projection can discard useful signal. No pretrained embedding, VAE or supervised feature fitting enters the primary hybrid. Register the adaptation in the source register before experimental use. See Blundell source S3 for the antecedent and its limits.

## F03 amendment proposal: history-valid en-passant family

Preserve the impossible original family and its diagnosis. En-passant cannot reply to a capture, since its prerequisite is an immediately preceding double pawn advance. Proposed replacement: **initial en-passant capture followed by ordinary recapture onto the capturing pawn's destination**. Keep safe-capture definition and both existing certificate algorithms unchanged. Preserve 60 locked pairs, 30 new-kind pairs and five new kinds; propose 6 independent game pairs per new kind, since equal allocation was not itself an original threshold. No new specimens are generated here.

Acceptance before sealing: reconstruct the initial en-passant right from each source game's move history; execute the candidate, verify the third-square pawn removal and enumerate every legal reply onto the destination; replay both twins' legal histories independently. A hand-edited FEN is insufficient provenance. Document how the counterfactual twin is reached legally, family membership, no train/validation overlap and no duplicate originating game. Use a separately implemented rule cross-check for exceptional moves: two scripts sharing chess.js are not independent chess-law validation. If six valid pairs cannot be obtained, stop and ask for a revised family allocation before any sealing, rather than changing the count or definition silently. This is a downstream test-family defect; it is not evidence that Eyes rendering fails.

## F04 amendment proposals: attainable measurement without invented score signs

Original frozen requirement uses a 0.2/1.8 gain intervention and a 10%-of-token usable-strength ratio. Existing inspected gain rules cannot exceed 1. Current five-type coefficients are incomplete. Neither defect is resolved by the engineered store. The prior correction draft's statement that the memory is simply a linear KC readout is also too broad: downstream recurrent dynamics/saturation can make gain-to-endpoint preference nonlinear. A failed linear decoder rejects this decoder under its budget, not all information or all possible physiological access.

**F04-A, proposed minimal diagnostic registration:** keep 48 game-disjoint twin pairs, L2 logistic C=1, whole-game held-out evaluation, Graph/Raw-eyes/Tokens and the original count gates below. Fit feature scaling within each training fold only. Specify grouped folds, bootstrap seed and tie handling before data. Replace 1.8 by 1.0 only in a newly approved intervention version, applying the same range to Graph and Tokens. For each KC decoder coefficient w_i (training-fold fit only), define a synthetic decoder-aligned gain contrast: g_plus=1 when w_i>0 and .2 when w_i<0; g_minus reverses these; zero coefficients get equal gains. Apply the two vectors to an explicitly enumerated retained KC→observed-MBON edge mask in a diagnostic copy. Record all five raw type-mean differences, with no approach/avoid assignment.

Proposed scalar diagnostic M is the L2 norm of that five-component type-mean difference, evaluated on held-out twin presentations and averaged per game. Ratio M_graph/M_tokens must be >=.10 to retain the original numerical ratio as a **newly defined engineering sensitivity measure**, not a claim of identical old-gate meaning. A zero/invalid token denominator is a failed measurement, never infinity. This jointly bounded corner is not proven reachable by the learning dynamics and need not be a global upper bound in a recurrent model. Call it an admissible-gain sensitivity intervention with oracle decoder directions. Fold-wise labels never set production coefficients. Complete state restore after every intervention; frozen unperturbed records remain separate. The mask, gain indexing and handling of shared gains must pass static review before this becomes executable.

This makes a measurable, attainable-parameter diagnostic, but deliberately does **not** license the original biological usable-choice claim. The researcher must approve both the new statistic and narrower interpretation. No existing run can pass retroactively. An isolated gain corner cannot show real acquisition.

**F04-B, proposed hybrid-route alternative:** use the read-only decoder result to characterize the input, then test the declared nearest-neighbour hybrid directly in the feedback-enabled development study below. Judge useful access by its unaided all-legal held-out choices and component controls, not by a hypothetical MBON gain threshold. This is a separately approved Volume 3 route and retains original eye-test gates as unresolved for the biological route. It is not a silent waiver. Prefer B if reasoning is the adopted priority; choose A only if the physiological-access question materially blocks a stated next feedback study. Neither proposal needs complete biological-memory repair.

**E2: Phase B transfer of prior learning.** After E1, approve a new task and sealed families. Prior Phase A memory versus fresh versus Phase A shuffled-exposure at equal total practice; add exact retrieval and raw-input learned/retrieval baselines. **E3: Phase C minimal composition.** After A/B, choose two distinct concepts (candidate: safe capture and a bounded mating constraint). Two is the minimum and falls short of "many unrelated concepts". Each must first generalize separately. Lock tasks requiring both; counterfactuals change one relevant consequence while retaining misleading surface similarity. No combination training, concept tags, test feedback or memory writes. Compare all-concepts, each single-concept, fresh, shuffled, lookup-only, same-controller raw/random/rewired representations and memory-component ablations. Equalize total training slots with task-neutral exposures rather than giving the combined arm twice the practice. Auditor certifies that removing either concept changes the required solution; reject tasks solvable by one criterion alone. Multi-ply mate claims require complete bounded defense certificates and earlier-choice attribution; exhausted verification remains unknown. If a controller hardcodes conjunction, attribute combination to that controller unless learned-component interventions establish otherwise.

**E4: connectome attribution, conditional on useful E1 performance.** Build prospectively seeded degree/sign/weight-distribution-matched rewiring controls in isolated copies, preserving the released graph unchanged. Propose at least 3 independently sampled rewires for development to reveal null-model variability; this is a feasibility minimum, not a sufficient estimate of the full topology null. Preserve sensory/output identities and document which degree, type, spatial and compartment constraints the null preserves. Compare with raw eyes, matched random sparse features and direct KC route. Evaluate saturation/dynamic range so a trivially disconnected or silent null cannot make the released graph look uniquely useful. Freeze identical unsupervised scaling rules per architecture; never normalize using final labels.

Evidence for topology's contribution requires reproducible held-out benefit over these matched controls under the same learner, plus a predeclared pathway intervention that removes the advantage and restoration that restores it without generic signal collapse. Retain an unrelated size/activity-matched lesion. Source-supported intervention targets must be chosen before inspecting test outcomes. A representation-only benefit supports an inductive bias supplied by this wiring in this implementation. A benefit of live neural-score use beyond the same graph-derived descriptor+memory controller is additionally required to credit online neural decision computation. Neither establishes evolved chess machinery or living-fly reasoning. A fair negative result is also publishable evidence of limits, subject to biological insight and venue review.

## Replication, uncertainty, resource plan and stop rules

The independent sampling unit for position generalization is the originating game/family, with all twins, moves and transformations clustered together. Moves, frames, episodes and repeated snapshots are not independent n. The implementation is one graph/anatomical specimen. Training-order/choice seeds measure algorithmic variation; rewiring seeds measure null-topology variation. Three learner runs required by Phase A do not estimate variation across flies.

Use paired game-cluster bootstrap intervals for accuracy differences, retaining within-game covariance between twins/arms. Proposed 10,000 bootstrap resamples and 95% intervals; predeclare PRNG and endpoint aggregation. Publish each training-seed estimate and its paired interval; for an across-seed summary use a crossed game/seed resampling analysis as descriptive sensitivity, acknowledging unstable seed variance at n=3. Report all gates per seed rather than treating 180 repeated pairs as independent. Both-member correct ordering, all-legal safe-choice rate, counterfactual flip correctness, new-family performance, unknown fraction, retrieval coverage/distance, retention and audit failures are separate endpoints. Report denominators and ties. New endpoints have effect sizes and uncertainty; Phase B/C require independent numerical approval before running. For many comparator contrasts, propose simultaneous bootstrap lower bounds or Holm-controlled tests at familywise .05 in addition to the existing marginal-interval gates; do not silently replace the original gates. No post hoc winner or optional stopping on favorable intervals.

Replication choice: retain the frozen three order/choice runs for Phase A. Propose the same three development seeds across every E1 arm as an initial feasibility panel, then a fresh three-seed confirmation registration after development/validation freeze. Development is not confirmation. These numbers constrain laptop cost; there is no estimated effect distribution or power analysis. Before final confirmation, use development-only cluster variance to present achievable precision and minimum effect of interest to the researcher; if the budget cannot resolve it, narrow the claim or explicitly label the study inconclusive. Do not keep adding seeds until significance appears.

Resource ceilings from repository authority: sparse graph, one position and one graph process at a time, heap 256 MiB, RSS <=2048 MiB and free system memory >=256 MiB, duty .5. Frozen old runs keep their old ceilings. The 128×5177 float32 descriptor payload is approximately 2.53 MiB; raw-eye 128×5543 is 2.71 MiB, excluding indexing, copies, model and logs. Bound temporary buffers and store one arm at a time. Stream receipts. No dense adjacency or edge-by-batch array, workers, GPU or new dependency.

The prior 3,318-forward live pilot took 829.15 s and peak sampled RSS 257.91 MiB; its approximately .25 s/forward is workload-specific, not an eye-route timing promise. Estimate cost as arms×seeds×(candidate forwards+teacher/delay/evaluation forwards), where legal candidate count varies by board. Proposed initial development ceiling: 5,400 s per serial job (borrowed as an engineering cap from the pilot), then stop and publish incomplete status if reached. Total study hours remain unestimated until the actual registered candidate counts and a separately authorized bounded profile exist. Fit the scientific design to a declared total budget before launch; a per-job cap must not conceal unlimited jobs. Measure CPU, wall time, RSS sampling interval and memory-store size. Resource failure is incomplete/unknown evidence, not failure/reward; resume only by the registered recovery contract.

Go only after approved contract, source registration, software/identity checks, authoritative process check, informative arm ready and untouched train/validation/final boundaries. Stop on certificate/state/hash mismatch, missing observer identity, exceeded guard, unexpected writes during inference or unobservable input conflict. Stop at frozen fail/grey/shortcut outcomes; no renderer/readout tuning on spent final families. Lookup-only equality means no incremental neural claim. Raw/simple matching means no demonstrated need for the connectome. A rewired match prevents crediting detailed topology. Combination matching a single concept prevents a combination claim. Failure of one candidate/budget never proves universal incapacity.
