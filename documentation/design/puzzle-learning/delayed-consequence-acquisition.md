# Delayed-consequence acquisition requirements

2026-10-07. Development specification; no frozen learning protocol or completed transfer experiment.

The [all-legal certificate audit](../../../archive/early-development/research/reports/delayed-transfer-solvability-2026-10-07/README.md) confirms that the existing rook-concession fixture has an immediate mating alternative. It remains useful for teacher replay and explicit root-action feedback, but accuracy on it cannot demonstrate learning to investigate a delayed concession. The inspected support counterfactual is also development evidence, not untouched final testing.

Before a delayed-concept experiment, construct and independently certify training positions where no immediate mating alternative satisfies the task. Verify a successful strategy with the concession and adequate defensive coverage. A material loss on one illustrative branch alone does not establish that the concession is necessary. Record all legal root outcomes, including unknowns, and explicitly check shorter horizons.

Train on bounded experiences generated by the existing seeded Monte Carlo planner, with teacher verification after commitment and memory frozen during investigation. Report omissions, mistaken assessment, missed defensive replies and depth cutoffs separately. Replay only verified contexts through the existing identified reinforcement interface; unknown outcomes supply no event. Immediate replay is explicit experimental root credit, not a validated long-delay eligibility mechanism. A richer explanation stored in a report is not evidence the neural receiver interpreted it.

Use matched absent, shuffled and disabled-plasticity exposure controls, all-legal unaided assessments, and separately fixed-search-budget assessments. Publish memory plus pending receipts atomically and audit reset/restoration and resumed update equivalence. Keep the declared physiological rule and readout fixed for the experiment.

Generate disjoint training, validation and final-test families before neural selection. Previously inspected compositions, simple mirrored copies and encoder-enforced symmetries cannot serve as independent final transfer evidence. Include unseen successful delayed positions and counterfactuals where the tempting sacrifice fails but a verified alternative exists. Select checkpoints only on per-criterion validation gates; freeze the selected memory before final testing. Require improvement above controls and choices that track the changed consequences. These requirements preserve the original reasoning goal; a familiar repair remains an intermediate milestone.
