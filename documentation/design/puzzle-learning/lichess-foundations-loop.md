# Lichess foundations and the automated teaching loop

21 October 2026. Active implementation direction, **not an executable full curriculum or a scientific pass**. Implements the researcher's request for `fly babysit` to manage puzzle practice, feedback, rehearsal, saving and gated progression across the twelve planned stages. It does not guarantee the model can learn every stage. See the [frozen curriculum](principles-curriculum.md), [active scope](../../handoffs/curriculum-reasoning-goal.md) and [current handoff](../../handoffs/curriculum-reasoning-state.md).

## Completion requirements

1. A reproducible curriculum maps each stage to learnable relationships, a precise consequence task, complete opponent coverage and a bounded independent verifier.
2. One command supervises registered practice and causal controls, resumes complete checkpoints, rehearses earlier knowledge, selects through validation and advances only after independent stage acceptance. Every failed, unknown or unavailable gate saves an explicit stopped state.
3. Outcome-only and separately registered Stockfish-assisted teaching are implemented without importing chess weights or letting a teacher change neural parameters.
4. Learned foundations survive interference and support unaided useful decisions on unfamiliar counterfactual families and previously untrained combinations. Frozen gates, controls and independent audits apply; puzzle rating or familiar repair cannot substitute.
5. A preserved evidence bundle establishes the actual scope reached. A working controller, synthetic pass, teacher proof or strong external engine alone cannot complete the reasoning goal.

## Pillars and stage meaning

The computational demands are interacting objects, conditional relations, opponent resources, delayed consequences, retention and composition. Human chess topic names organize experiences; they do not establish that the model has those computational capabilities. Board occupancy and candidate descriptions are public inputs. Teacher-computed attacks, pin flags, engine values, source solution and themes must not supply the answer before unaided ranking. Legal-action filtering is engineered rules infrastructure and must be included in shortcut analysis.

| Stage | Relationship and falsifying contrast | Consequence boundary and readiness |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Attack, defense and recapture | The same capture becomes recapturable when a defender's line opens or its king constraint changes. | Existing all-legal capture-retention proof/checker; at most six pieces. Retaining the capturer is a task outcome, not a claim of best chess play. G0 and full stage gates remain unmet. |
| 2. King safety, check and mate | A check stops being mate when a legal escape, capture or interposition becomes available. | Existing bounded-mate proof/checker; certify each proposed action against every required defense. A move absent from the legal mask cannot alone demonstrate learned check avoidance. |
| 3. Sound forcing moves and reply awareness | An apparently forcing action fails when a quiet defensive resource is restored. | Bounded mate/material tasks, including quiet opponent moves. Material proof support and complete all-legal stage runner remain required. |
| 4. Double threats and overloaded defense | A geometric fork fails when one reply neutralizes both targets or removes the attacker. | Complete bounded payoff proof. Forcing-only opponent sampling cannot establish this claim. Include matching poisoned or jointly defensible double attacks. |
| 5. Lines, blockers and removal of defenders | A preparatory change helps only when it changes a defender relation and enables a checked payoff. | Builds on Stage 1 blockers/pins; adds preparatory sequence credit. Preserve earlier tests rather than counting the same static relation twice. |
| 6. Move order, intermediate moves and sacrifices | Reordering otherwise similar actions changes whether the eventual payoff survives defense. | Explicit delayed-credit mechanism and complete bounded certificates required. No assigning an entire line's reward to every earlier move by assumption. |
| 7. Piece effectiveness | Improving a piece enables a verified task outcome; the same mobility increase without that consequence is insufficient. | Prospective controlled tasks only until a bounded consequence domain is implemented. An engine preference alone cannot establish a general positional principle. |
| 8. Coordination and shared duties | Removing support or imposing a second duty defeats a previously sound sequence. | Immediate coordination can reuse earlier tactical proofs. Longer coordination requires separately verified horizons and interference tests. |
| 9. Weaknesses, pawn structure and weak squares | A weakness matters when exploitation works; compensating resources can reverse that result. | No general exact positional verifier exists. Define restricted certificate-backed tasks first; keep Stockfish value comparisons separately labeled. |
| 10. Restriction, prophylaxis and the opponent's plan | Preventing an actual verified threat helps; matched positions without that threat should not elicit the same action preference. | Immediate prevention is an earlier-stage assay. Longer plans need independent horizon/credit/working-memory evidence, not assumed lookahead. |
| 11. Pawn breaks, exchanges and endgame transitions | The same irreversible choice changes consequence with king activity, support or a defensive reply. | Restricted exact tasks or declared oracle comparisons only until implemented. Tablebase or teacher search is not internal learner planning. |
| 12. Compositional plans and selective adversarial investigation | Independently learned relations must be combined on new families; changing a prerequisite must reverse the decision. | Separate unaided composition and fixed-budget investigation. Hold combinations out of training and validation. Do not train the final combinations and call them zero-shot. |

These retain the twelve reader-facing stage numbers and existing internal IDs. Stages 7–11 are research targets with unresolved verifiers, not ready training jobs. Unsupported stages must stop with a readiness reason. A shorter foundational study may test the earliest tractable composition, but it does not count as completing all twelve.

## Source-to-experience path

1. Stream the [CC0 Lichess archive](lichess-puzzle-corpus.md) into bounded candidate samples. Ratings/themes select experiences on the teacher side; they are not targets or rewards. A daily API response is a small development fixture, not a corpus acquisition strategy.

   Before preparing training/validation lessons, run the [independent candidate-source audit](../../../.codebase/scripts/data/audit-lichess-source-candidates.mjs). It verifies selected rows against the full pinned CSV, legal setup/source-line replay, source-game split, piece/count limits and archive/extraction hashes. It does not inspect `final.jsonl` or certify relational-family separation, full history or teaching consequences.21 bounded source/history/planner/certificate checks pass; the real corpus audit must still run after its curator publishes.
2. Recover standard-start source PGN and verify setup move, ply, solver side and position. For CSV, use the documented pre-setup FEN and first move. For API, check its position and setup against replay; do not overwrite real repetition history or clocks with reset API counters. Reject HTML, truncated/inconsistent histories, unsupported starting positions and illegal lines.
3. Build separate public context `{fen,pgn,task}` and private source/proof records. The public PGN ends at the decision; future solution moves and tags stay private. Move descriptions must not contain consequences.
4. Recompute every legal action under the registered task and required opponent coverage. Exhausted proof is unknown and produces no event. Do not shrink a full source position to meet a piece limit while pretending to preserve its source-game history. A derived miniature needs a new explicit synthetic-history contract and is reported separately.
5. Group source games, transpositions, symmetry equivalents and related counterfactual/distractor variants into families before assigning training/validation/final splits. A source-game hash alone is insufficient. Final families remain sealed from training, error targeting and implementation selection.

Implemented so far: [history binding](../../../.codebase/src/puzzle-learning/lichess-history.mjs), [capture lesson preparation](../../../.codebase/src/puzzle-learning/lichess-capture-lesson.mjs), [training-panel bridge](../../../.codebase/src/puzzle-learning/lichess-curriculum-panel.mjs) and [bounded preparation CLI](../../../.codebase/scripts/data/prepare-lichess-lessons.mjs). The bridge accepts only registered training assignments, checks every legal-action certificate, then captures a fresh all-legal neural ranking with ties recorded as no unique choice. It returns the observation shape consumed by the rehearsal planner. Its caller still has to verify the pinned family assignment and corpus source audit. Prepared candidates remain inert until the receiver gate and runnable stage protocol authorize teaching. Static counterfactuals derived from those roots need a separate provenance mode; they must not inherit the authentic PGN as though the changed board were played. See the [synthetic-position contract](synthetic-position-provenance.md). It is a proposal only; the current panel binder still requires an authentic standard-start prefix.

The [registered Python source-PGN fetcher](../../../python%20actions/fetch_lichess_game_pgns.py) uses Lichess's documented batch export endpoint, checks requested versus returned game IDs, and writes per-game PGNs plus an atomic manifest. Its one-game development probe is preserved; no train/validation corpus fetch has run. ## Feedback and the model's own evaluation

The primary condition teaches independently certified outcomes. A failed capture-retention objective does not mean a lost game; failure to force mate within a horizon does not mean the opponent wins. Keep criteria separate rather than imposing an unjustified universal ordering such as material loss versus allowing mate at incompatible horizons.

The researcher also authorizes Stockfish-assisted feedback. An isolated prototype exists in the linked [Stockfish-target value-learning design](stockfish-value-learning.md). It defines a fixed, uncalibrated MBON value proxy, binds the engine target to the chosen move's child board/history, corrects actor perspective, requires independently verified task evidence and supports outcome-only, informative, no-channel, shuffled and disabled conditions. It uses existing local plasticity for a bounded engineered error signal; this does not validate that signal as biological dopamine or establish a calibrated evaluator. Historical adapter checks are documented there and in current-status.md; no Stockfish graph-learning run has occurred.

The Stage 1 runtime factory can collect its own engine reviews when the registration supplies `stockfishCollection` instead of a pre-collected `stockfishReviews` file. The collection registration pins an absolute executable path and SHA256, arguments, matched node budgets, timeout/deadline, hash size and a cache directory under `runs/`. It analyzes every legal child from the exact source history, then binds the results to the existing independently checked task certificates. Raw UCI input/output is preserved per panel. Each completed panel review is atomically published and reused after interruption; a partial UCI attempt is preserved and logged before a new attempt begins. Training does not start until all registered panels have complete reviews. This is software integration only: the cache path has not been exercised with a real Stockfish executable, and it does not resolve the missing family, acceptance or stage-readiness gates.

Stage 1 matrix auditing must use a separately pinned evaluator module to derive acceptance observations from the completed matrix, independently replayed lineages and registered disjoint evaluation data. A static observation file cannot be pinned in the registration while also containing the resulting matrix digest and registration digest: both values exist only after execution, and the registration hash would change when the file hash is added. The evaluator module and its fixed data/protocol inputs can be pinned before training; its derived observations are then bound to the exact matrix result, registration and each condition/replica's replayed initial/final memory hashes. The separate gate recomputes frozen D1-D8 thresholds; matrix replay alone cannot manufacture validation/final choices or mark a gate passed. The production evaluator and required validation/final family packages are still absent, so this is an integration contract, not a functioning promotion pipeline.

An accepted stage must also publish a per-lineage `promotionMemoryHashes` map that identifies the checkpoint selected by the frozen validation procedure. The independent matrix auditor requires this map and its recorded frozen-selection evidence whenever `stageAccepted` is true. The Python campaign verifies the training-final hashes against full replay, but carries the validation-selected hashes into the next stage; it preserves the last-training hashes separately in campaign state. Final-family evaluation must happen only after the validation selection is frozen. The selection evaluator is not implemented yet, so no current stage can be promoted through this path.

The pure Stage 1 selector now requires a contiguous checkpoint sequence from block 0, the same 16 validation trials with exactly four per semantic stratum at every checkpoint, and the frozen D2 gate (at least 14/16 correct and at least 3/4 in every stratum). Among eligible checkpoints it chooses highest total correct, then the best worst-stratum count, then the fewest training blocks. It accepts no final data. The runtime factory now supplies a fresh evaluation arm from each pinned result lineage to the separately pinned evaluator; the evaluator that scores every checkpoint, runs this selector, freezes the winner and only then opens final data is still missing.

The versioned [Stage 1 acceptance gate v2](../../../.codebase/src/puzzle-learning/curriculum-stage1-acceptance-v2.mjs) preserves the original D1-D8 decision rule and adds D9: the informative outcome-plus-Stockfish condition must beat both the matched outcome-only arm and the matched shuffled-Stockfish arm by at least 10 final choices, with a positive paired family-bootstrap 95% lower bound. This makes Stockfish assistance a separately tested contribution instead of assuming that a registered arm proves value. The stricter gate has not been run; it still requires the missing disjoint evaluation families and observation producer.

Before a graph experiment, the campaign still needs:

- Pinned engine binary/version/network, options, threads/hash, limits and target perspective. Freeze and evaluate the current fixed transform and max-over-action proxy as hypotheses, not calibrated value.
- A complete matched training-only target/error schedule, generated and pinned before graph exposure. Preserve exact board/history/action, task certificate, engine transcript and all-legal coverage.
- Integration with the registered serial curriculum runner, including resource guards, atomic pending receipts, exact recovery and independent neural replay. Refresh source hashes for the new protocol.
- Matched outcome-only, outcome-plus-Stockfish, absent, shuffled and disabled-plasticity arms. Keep certified outcomes separate from engine estimates.
- Unaided choices and fixed-search-budget evaluation, plus held-out validation, retention and disjoint counterfactual transfer. Engine search/source solutions cannot rescue a failing unaided gate. The generic punishment/omission mechanism cannot accept arbitrary signed updates outside the frozen interface.

## Repeated practice and rehearsal

Reuse the frozen curriculum's 32 four-event blocks per stage: current failed training action, separately certified successful alternative, earlier successful sentinel, earlier failed counterexample. This is 128 known teaching events and 50% rehearsal per condition/replica; Stage 1 rehearses another Stage 1 pair. Retarget from informative-arm **training** errors at the registered cadence. If an error was repaired, use the predeclared uncovered-pair rule rather than inventing a current failure.

Yoke controls to the informative arm's board/action/proof/order/timing sequence. Independently recheck each proof before replay. Unknown/stale proofs or missing balanced strata stop the block. Fresh unaided all-legal ranking must bind to the exact current memory, history and task before each event. Existing temporal sessions provide frame transactions; disk publication, receipts and recovery remain the runner's responsibility.

Implemented [rehearsal planner](../../../.codebase/src/puzzle-learning/curriculum-rehearsal-plan.mjs): six bounded cases validate current-error selection, honest mastered/tied fallback, saved exposure counts, training-only/all-legal/memory checks, unknown/missing-balance stops, prior-stage rehearsal and the32-block ceiling. It assigns outcomes, not biological channels. `nextSelectionCounts` is a proposed post-block state and must be committed only after durable block completion. The caller must authenticate the frozen panel/family registration and reassess every neural event. This planner alone does not run, save or accept a learning stage.

The [Lichess block bridge](../../../.codebase/src/puzzle-learning/lichess-curriculum-block.mjs) now connects registered panels, fresh all-legal choices, current-memory rehearsal planning and the resumable temporal block runner. The block checkpoint includes the exact selection plan, including the pre-block checkpoint, so recovery does not have to infer or regenerate it. A four-cell integration check passes through the panel-to-feedback path. This is still a software fixture; there is no registered stage caller, real-graph learner run or scientific stage acceptance.

The [block session](../../../.codebase/src/puzzle-learning/curriculum-block-session.mjs) now connects that plan to the existing temporal receiver. A [one-block runner](../../../.codebase/src/puzzle-learning/curriculum-block-runner.mjs) publishes immutable compressed snapshots with pending receipts before acknowledgement, preserves parent runs and resumes into a new directory. Event-start records contain the fresh all-legal ranking. Its exact record/publication ceiling follows the registered frame schedule (at most137 records/275 publications,16 MiB per checkpoint,1 MiB per receipt,64 MiB log). The caller must supply pinned identities, assessment, resource guard and pacing; this library does not authorize an experiment.

A [separate replay path](../../../.codebase/src/puzzle-learning/curriculum-block-audit.mjs) reconstructs block bookkeeping without calling the runner/coordinator transitions, recomputes unaided rankings and neural frames, and checks the final publication. [Twenty-one software checks](../../../runs/python-actions/20261021T121643-84121c43/stdout.txt) use four rate-array cells and synthetic reset-history boards. They cover12 interrupted disk recoveries, all controls, changed rankings/records, stale assessments, longer schedules and a later block. They do not establish physical power-loss survival, graph-scale resources, biological learning, valid relation-family splits or stage acceptance. Curriculum-wide orchestration and the scientific acceptance auditor remain required.

Do not train forever until a percentage rises. Exposure limits and checkpoint-selection rules are frozen before a run. New doses or mechanisms require a new registration, preserved negative results and matched controls. Validation selects per-criterion snapshots; it does not supply teaching events. Do not roll back within a measured interference challenge to conceal forgetting.

## `fly babysit` progression contract

The existing single-run mode supervises one registered job through its independent audit. A campaign option now extends that path with a pinned twelve-stage registration:

1. Read the selected curriculum registration, owned process, protocol pins, accepted parent and last complete publication. Unknown ownership blocks launch; a lock or elapsed wait does not establish termination.
2. Resolve the next stage by its explicit prerequisites. If its teacher, receiver, data/family audit, runner, resource registration or independent acceptance auditor is unavailable, save `readiness-blocked` with the missing evidence and stop.
3. Run registered practice, controls and computational replicas serially. Publish persistent memory plus pending receipt before acknowledging an update; resume exactly once from a complete checkpoint into a new directory.
4. Run the independent acceptance auditor. Audit-integrity pass and scientific-stage pass are separate. A zero process exit or `passed=true` from a runner cannot promote a snapshot.
5. Promote only a snapshot whose full applicable D1–D8 and stage-specific gates pass, with earlier retention intact and complete evidence hashes. Advance only to an already registered compatible stage. An acceptance failure saves `gate-failed`; missing/exhausted evidence saves `unknown`. Do not automatically change biological mechanisms, shuffle seeds, thresholds or datasets.
6. Resume the same campaign with the same command after an ordinary interruption. A scientific/readiness stop needs a newly reviewed registration before dependent work can continue. Preserve the failed campaign rather than reusing its final families.

Bare `fly babysit` selects the default campaign registration; `fly babysit --curriculum PATH` selects one explicitly. The controller verifies stage ordering, stage-config hashes, prerequisite-file hashes and declared prerequisite booleans; then calls the existing train-to-audit supervisor serially and accepts a stage only when every registered `audit.*` boolean is exactly `true`. Campaign state is atomic and binds to the registration hash. It stops on unavailable inputs, mismatches, unknown gate values or false gates. `fly --config PATH babysit` remains the explicit single-run mode. This is orchestration only: no complete twelve-stage registration currently binds the Lichess lessons, feedback arms, block runner, receiver, retention/composition gates and independent auditors. Until it exists, bare `fly babysit` reports readiness-blocked without launching the default G0 experiment.

The final campaign also requires unfamiliar combinations, causal state removal/restoration, retention, shortcut baselines and independent replay. Report actual domains reached and limitations. `all-stages-complete` requires all twelve accepted stages; the broader reasoning claim additionally requires the registered composition evidence.
