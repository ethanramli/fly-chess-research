# Feedback quality improvement contract

2026-10-08. Researcher-directed active work. [Primary-source review](../../../research/literature/feedback-quality-deep-review-2026-10-08/review.md). No effective-learning or 8/10 result yet. Preserve all frozen protocols and failures.

## Required outcome

Increase retained, useful, consequence-sensitive unfamiliar choices per verified experience and total compute while preserving from-scratch connectome-constrained endogenous learning. Better records alone do not satisfy this outcome. MiMo, Stockfish, Capablanca and biological literature supply hypotheses, not a replacement learner.

## Stage A: Checked lesson contract

Build on existing independently checked contrastive review, sequence planner and pre-feedback ranking binder. Every lesson must record exact board/history/action/task, independent certificate and scope, unaided ranking/coverage and memory hash, verified consequence, responsible-context limits, intended teaching channel and the actual information the receiver consumes. A prose explanation is teacher evidence only. Unknown, forgery, context mismatch, incomplete defense coverage or proof exhaustion gives no teaching event. Preserve child-board actor perspective and task/search separation.

Separate certified task truth from finite-budget engine estimates. Keep engine utility, regret, prediction error, mate deadline and efficiency distinct. Saturated WDL cannot replace task proof. An engine estimate condition requires pinned settings, full legal coverage, valid latest output and declared uncertainty handling. No target fitting on final families.

## Stage B: Direction and specificity gate

Predeclare paired successful/failed candidate contexts from training, with nuisance variation and consequence-changing relations. Measure pair margin before and after each actual verified feedback event; subtract no-channel drift. Report every candidate's score movement, including common offsets and regression. For value teaching require target error to decrease over matched controls, not just current/efficacy change. Deliver identical informative experiences/order/timing to absent, nondegenerate matched-shuffled and disabled controls. Match both channel counts and signed magnitudes where applicable. A one-event opposite-valence control is not a count-balanced shuffle.

Do not change a completed assay's current, polarity, dose, seed or rule to force a pass. A new justified mechanism must be separately sourced, registered and tested for bounded association, persistence and reversal before a broader claim. Extra wiring, pretrained heads, backprop and teacher-generated gain deltas remain excluded.

## Stage C: Useful repair and transfer

Freeze train, validation and untouched final families before graph use. Measure all-legal unaided choices; candidate-pair ranking is a diagnostic only. Select checkpoints only with per-criterion validation, requiring retained useful choices and no required-criterion worsening. Reset removes the acquired effect; restore recovers it. Freeze learning during final evaluation. Require independent unfamiliar layouts and counterfactual reversals, shortcut/memorization baselines and functional replication factors. Immediate retries are repair only. Do not reuse inspected v3 final families as final evidence.

## Stage D: Multi-ply feedback and economical investigation

After the relevant input-to-choice bridge is demonstrated, compare root-only with fixed-budget checked earlier-context replay. Sequence credit requires explicit verified scope; an illustrative line is not causal necessity or best-defense proof. Encoder/task compatibility must be versioned. Compare outcome-only with separately validated Stockfish-value teaching. Preserve quiet moves and sacrifices. Measure unaided and equal-search choices separately. Order candidates before restricting coverage; missing defenses remain unknown.

Capablanca-derived curriculum: blocked/open activity, competing defensive duties, exchanges and endgame transitions, quiet preparation and justified sacrifice. Each needs exceptions and independently checked outcomes; activity/simplicity is not an unconditional reward. Search efficiency is secondary to correctness and tactical safety.

## Quality reporting

Maintain ten proposed yes/no properties: context binding, task-evidence alignment, checked contrast, credit scope, delivery fidelity, selective update, retained non-regression, unaided useful choices, disjoint/counterfactual transfer, measured compute efficiency. Report teacher quality and learner efficacy separately. Do not label the method 8/10 until at least eight are independently supported, with selectivity, retention, useful choices and transfer mandatory. This prospective reporting shorthand cannot redefine existing scientific gates.
