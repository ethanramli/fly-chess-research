# Lichess capture comparisons and data readiness

22 October 2026. Training-data work, not a qualified receiver, trained chess model or reasoning result.

## Why the first audited corpus is insufficient

The v7 preparation and independent audit cover 128 training and 128 validation histories and all legal immediate capture-retention consequences. They establish provenance and certificate integrity. The training-only contrast review found 20 roots with a successful capture, 13 with a failed capture and **zero with both**. Its 20 producer-eligible roots compare successful captures with quiet actions that fail the capture task. Choosing a capture can therefore exploit a shortcut.

A quiet task failure does not mean a bad chess move. Keep those labels unchanged. Teacher certification, useful within-root comparisons, disjoint relational families, receiver qualification and neural efficacy require separate evidence.

## Preserved legal-continuation attempts

Two registered teacher-side searches appended a common legal quiet move and alternative quiet opponent-king moves to actual training histories. Acceptance required the same legal choices and material, only the opposing king changing location, and opposite unique successful capture choices. These continuations would be declared generated branches, never falsely described as source-game play or internal Fly lookahead.

- King-only common moves: recorded 512 branches across 39 roots; zero families; branch bound reached.
- Any quiet common move with a two-target geometry prefilter: recorded 277 branches across 103 roots; zero families; 300-second bound reached.

These are bounded negative construction results. Search coverage is producer-recorded; no independently audited real family was produced. Synthetic branch construction and a separate derivation/certificate checker pass their software checks. The raw attempts remain preserved.

## Current training-only source selection

The new source-candidate condition requires:

1. At most six pieces after the source setup move.
2. Exactly one capture whose moved piece has no immediate legal recapture.
3. A recapturable alternative by the **same moving piece**, capturing the **same piece type**.
4. No capture-promotion alternatives in this condition.

This is a preliminary **FEN-only filter**. It exports no consequence certificate and does not resolve repetition or source history. The normal source-game PGN retrieval, independent prefix reconstruction, all-legal proof preparation and replay must follow. A failed history check or exhausted proof cannot produce a neural event.

Only stable training source-game splits are inspected for chess/rating/theme selection. Excluded validation/final games are routed out before those semantic checks; existing reserved final JSONL stays unopened. Sampling uses a complete bounded scan of the existing pinned archive and a fixed 64-row reservoir/seed. This does not establish relational-family separation: transpositions, symmetries, related branches and later held-out combinations still require registration.

The matched captures remove an always-capture or fixed piece-value explanation for choosing the correct capture. Coordinate, positional and motif shortcuts still require controlled counterfactual and disjoint evaluation. Candidate enrichment alone does not establish reasoning.
