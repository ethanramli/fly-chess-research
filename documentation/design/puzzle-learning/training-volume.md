2026-10-06. Researcher asked whether insufficient volume, rather than a defective learning mechanism, explains the failures, and warned against assuming human-like plasticity. This is a plausible competing explanation. Four/eight-event fixture failures are evidence at those budgets; they do not establish incapacity at larger doses. The causal replay also does not establish that recovery is the unique problem. Preserve every failed gate without generalizing beyond its tested scope.

More **training experiences** can change learned state. More **independent test runs/seeds** improve confidence in an estimated effect but do not supply training to a frozen brain. Repeat counts, distinct experience coverage, feedback quality and simulated timing are separate variables. The current rate constants and numerical steps are unfitted assumptions, not measured human/fly plasticity or elapsed biological durations.

Primary evidence: [Jacob and Waddell, 2020, Neuron](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0896627320302208), DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2020.03.013, reports that repetitive spaced conditioning forms complementary aversive/safety memories, with safety-memory acquisition depending on repetition, order and spacing. This motivates testing dose and schedule, not importing that study's trial counts into chess or claiming our source-derived efficacy rule implements its consolidation pathways. Laptop duty-cycle rest is not simulated neural rest; no between-trial consolidation dynamics are currently implemented.
