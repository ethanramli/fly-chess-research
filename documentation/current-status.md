## Latency optimization status

The first measured optimization caches the derived signed edge magnitude used in sparse recurrent propagation. The later run peaked at 109.09 MiB RSS.

These are engineering latency results only. The signed-cache cycle is one fixture, not a full training-round comparison; neither it nor a shortlist closes the gap to hundreds of millions of evaluated positions or demonstrates faster learning per useful experience. A full-graph PUCT profile used a two-evaluation cap and completed one simulation. In two runs per mode, opt-in forcing selection reduced candidate cues from 48 to 32 and showed preliminary median CPU/runtime reductions, but every mode chose the same independently verified failure. A diagnostic four-edge unroll of the recurrent sum was exactly equivalent but slower than the cached signed-magnitude kernel in the measured microbenchmark, so it was not adopted. The cache adds 10.8 MB of derived state; internal magnitude mutation paths refresh it. External direct edits to `params.magnitude` must use `setMagnitude` or call `refreshSignedMagnitude` before inference. Frozen old experiment pins and archives were not rewritten; new runs must pin the updated sources.
