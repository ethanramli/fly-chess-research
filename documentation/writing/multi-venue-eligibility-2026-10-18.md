## eLife

The inspected official submission/peer-review pages impose no author age, degree or nationality criterion. Thus no published age/degree barrier was identified for a Singaporean high-school researcher in Indonesia; this is not a guarantee of submission-system access, minor contracting arrangements, review selection or publication. Use the actual school/independent affiliation; a mentor's institutional affiliation is not automatically the student's affiliation.

eLife expressly welcomes preprints, selects which submissions to send for expert review, and publishes reviewed work as a Reviewed Preprint with an assessment and public reviews. Authors can later finalize a Version of Record. This replaces the historical accept/reject-after-peer-review description in the September journal note. The current main submission page lists USD 3,750 and a waiver for inability to pay; an alternate retrieved author-FAQ page lists USD 3,000, so confirm the fee in the live submission system rather than treating either as a quote. No guaranteed turnaround or positive assessment is established.

The 2026 eLife Reviewed Preprint explanation explicitly permits authors to publish elsewhere after that stage. This is not blanket permission for simultaneous initial submission to multiple journals or for duplicate Versions of Record. Keep one journal submission active unless the relevant editors expressly permit an alternative. eLife's canonical detailed author-guide/policy pages returned access challenges; an exact current clause about initial concurrent journal review and prior conference abstracts remains unverified. The corresponding author should disclose every prior abstract/preprint/report and get confirmation for the actual sequence.

COSYNE's explicit non-full-proceedings policy and eLife's preprint support make conference dissemination plus a later eLife manuscript a sensible proposed route. Neither rule alone certifies every combination of venues. Before accepting full-paper proceedings or transferring copyright, check whether it would constitute prior publication of the intended journal article. An abstract DOI alone does not make an abstract a full research article.

## Earth Prize and excluded options

The Earth Prize 2027 accepts secondary students worldwide born 2007-2014; researcher meets the published age/location/status criteria. Registration closes 10 January; Blueprint closes 31 January 2027 at 12:00 CET (18:00 Jakarta). No general outside-competition exclusivity prohibition was located in the inspected rules, but portal declarations and publication/IP conditions still need confirmation for the proposed combination.

Its required scope is environmental sustainability. A finding about compact connectome-constrained computation could be scientifically important without yet being an environmental intervention. Brain size, RAM or a biological architecture alone does not establish reduced electricity use, emissions or comparable task quality. An environmental claim needs measured relevant impact; the current chess program has not established that impact. The research question remains intact: a result in an engineered fly-derived model would not by itself prove reasoning in living flies or show that present LLM tasks can be performed with the same resources.

ISPO is excluded from the recommended portfolio: its 2027 guide requires Indonesian citizenship and restricts manuscripts entered/currently selected elsewhere. Singaporean citizenship fails the first condition absent an explicit organizer exception. SSEF/National STEM Talent Search are not established routes from an Indonesian school: the published MOE-school requirement matters, and NSTS requires an SSEF award. Mentor involvement does not override nationality, school or publication rules.

## Compatible strategy and unresolved checks

Keep one research archive, with frozen datasets, raw outcomes, assistance/contribution records and versioned claims. Use it for distinct conference abstracts/posters, student competition reports and one full journal manuscript. Versions should transparently identify actual new results and prior dissemination, rather than conceal repeated work by changing titles. Keep final-test families sealed despite presentation deadlines. The currently documented G0 result remains failed at the reversal control gate; no new simulation or live-process check was performed here.

Priority: consider COSYNE only if the already-supported mechanistic result and author approvals justify the immediate deadline; otherwise prepare for CNS*2027, a genuinely later-progress SfN abstract, and eLife when the scientific study is ready. Treat ICYS and ISEF as conditional additions until entry routes are cleared. Earth Prize remains conditional on environmental fit, not personal age eligibility.
