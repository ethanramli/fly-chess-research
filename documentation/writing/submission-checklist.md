# Submission checklist

- [ ] The manuscript is framed as computational cognitive science, with chess serving as a testbed.
- [ ] The question, methods, evidence, and conclusions align; claims do not exceed the evidence.
- [ ] The distinction between the connectome-constrained model, external search, training signal, and interface is explicit.
- [ ] Held-out positions and leakage-safe novel-motif tests were defined before final evaluation.
- [ ] Matched baselines, search ablations, parameter/compute reporting, and multiple seeds are included.
- [ ] A high rating is not presented as proof of human-like cognition or biological fly understanding.
- [ ] Mentor/coauthors reviewed the final manuscript and approved submission.
- [ ] Author names, order, affiliations, and contribution statements are agreed and accurate.
- [ ] Ethics, consent, permissions, licenses, funding, and conflicts are disclosed as applicable.
- [ ] Appropriate reporting guidance for computational cognitive modeling has been identified.
- [ ] Data, code, configurations, checkpoints, and supplements match the sharing plan and venue requirements.
- [ ] The target venue fits cognitive science or computational neuroscience and its current instructions have been checked.
- [ ] A copy of the submitted version and correspondence is retained.
