# Manuscript outline

Use the structure expected in your field and target venue. Many empirical papers use IMRaD, but not every discipline does.

## Working title

## Abstract

Problem/context; objective; methods; main results; conclusion and implications. Write after the findings are clear, following the venue's format.

## Introduction

- What problem matters and to whom?
- What is established by the literature?
- What remains uncertain?
- What question/objective does this study address, and why is its approach appropriate?

## Methods

Design; setting/materials/participants; selection/recruitment; measures/procedures; ethics and permissions; analysis; transparency items required by the field.

## Results / findings

Report what the planned analysis shows, including relevant null or unexpected findings. Refer to clearly labeled tables and figures.

## Discussion

Interpretation in relation to prior work; contribution; practical or theoretical implications; strengths; limitations; next questions; conclusion proportionate to the evidence.

## Declarations and supporting material

Funding; conflicts; author contributions; ethics/consent; data/material/code availability; acknowledgements; references; supplementary files. Follow the journal's current requirements.
