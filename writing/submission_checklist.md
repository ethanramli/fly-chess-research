# Submission checklist

- [ ] The question, methods, evidence, and conclusions align; claims do not exceed the evidence.
- [ ] Mentor/coauthors reviewed the final manuscript and approved submission.
- [ ] Author names, order, affiliations, and contribution statements are agreed and accurate.
- [ ] Ethics, consent, permissions, trial/review registration, funding, and conflicts are disclosed as applicable.
- [ ] Appropriate reporting guideline for the design has been identified and completed where required.
- [ ] References and quotations are accurate; permissions/licenses are in place for reused material.
- [ ] Data, code, materials, and supplements match the sharing plan and venue requirements; no restricted information is exposed.
- [ ] Target venue fits the subject and article type; editorial board, peer review, fees, indexing claims, and policies have been checked using reliable sources.
- [ ] Current author instructions, formatting, word limits, and required files have been followed.
- [ ] Cover letter and declarations are accurate; this work is not simultaneously submitted elsewhere unless venue rules explicitly allow it.
- [ ] A copy of the submitted version and submission correspondence is retained.
