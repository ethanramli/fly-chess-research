# Curriculum completion audit

2026-10-11. Scope: the researcher requested a proper cumulative curriculum, targeting each principle, definitions of done and feedback that could support learning. This audit assesses the **design deliverable**. It does not certify that the proposed mechanism will learn, that its biology is validated, or that the curriculum is ready to launch.

| Requested element | Reviewable result | Design result |
|---|---|---|
| Start with a learnable elementary relationship | G0 tests association/retention/reversal, then Stage 1 tests legal recapturability; history-preserving teacher examples change one blocker. | Specified |
| Each first principle targeted explicitly | Twelve ordered lesson cards give definition, learning target, positive/negative experience, counterfactual, opponent coverage, task, horizon, capabilities and specialization. | Specified |
| Cumulative progression toward positional play | Stages 1-6 cover immediate consequences and tactical composition; Stages 7-11 cover effectiveness, coordination, weaknesses, prophylaxis and transitions; Stage 12 tests unseen combinations. Continuous memory, 50% rehearsal and later retention checks apply. | Specified |
| Operational definitions of done | D1-D8 fix acquisition, validation, unfamiliar choices, sign predictions, paired reversals, control advantage, interference retention, causal memory and independent replay. Each lesson adds its own strata; later value/episode gates supplement exact tasks. | Specified |
| Feedback explains what happened | Exact board/history/action/criterion certificates bind failed choice, defensive refutation and successful alternative. Unknown proof supplies no event. Evidence scope and horizon are explicit. | Specified |
| Feedback reaches persistent learning | F0 specifies the current cue/stimulus/local-rate recurrence interface; F1 requires sequence and earlier-choice credit validation; F2 defines a child-perspective neural prediction/error and proposed receiver; F3 binds actual terminal episodes and policy-scoped credit. Teacher never computes neural deltas. | Specified, with missing integrations flagged |
| Opponent moves and plans | Every lesson includes opposing actions. Selective play retains quiet refutations and widens within bounds; exhaustive teacher proof and all-legal diagnostics remain separate. | Specified |
| Protect causal inference and final testing | From-scratch matched arms, effective replicas, sealed disjoint families, shortcut checks, unknown handling, state removal/restoration and fixed unaided/search budgets. | Specified |
| Laptop-compatible execution and persistence | Sparse single-position serial graph, heap256/duty0.5, RSS768/free256 guards, bounded calls/frames, streamed receipts and atomic pending-update recovery. | Specified |
| Honest implementation status and source provenance | Actual capability paths and preserved failures are listed. Primary-source coverage distinguishes evidence from curriculum hypotheses. | Specified |

[Runnable specification check](verify-specification.mjs) verifies dependency order, quotas/thresholds, reference/path existence, unknown/no-delta boundaries and unchanged resource constraints. It must reject a cyclic prerequisite, impossible gate and teaching on unknown. It also constructs two illustrative capture certificates, independently replays all legal replies, rejects omitted replies and confirms exhausted verification produces no teaching permission. [Raw audit](audit.json) and [teacher examples](teacher-examples.json) record the results. These fixtures are excluded from training, validation and final testing; no neural activity is generated.

Manual implementation cross-check used the existing forcing shortlist, alternating Monte Carlo path, neutral mapped position value, capture/mate generator/checker and verified-feedback dispatch, temporal feedback plan/session, rate-timing memory, verified lesson frames and isolated Stockfish adapter. The current capture checker accepts captures only; supported mate dispatch is 1/3/5/7 plies. Missing material/safety/promotion proof, full-action capture handling, selective assessment binding, candidate-free calibrated position value, graded receiver and sequence/episode credit are **prerequisites**, not features claimed by this specification.

G0 and all twelve lessons remain unpassed. No graph launch, trained curriculum, biological retention, transferable reasoning, measured Elo or trained browser policy follows from a completed design. Fresh frozen stage protocols, disjoint manifests, reviewed receiver/verifier integrations and independent preflight are required before training. The earlier capture/value transfer failures remain intact.

Known limits: fixed sample counts/doses/gates are prospective engineering choices, not empirically optimized pedagogy. Certificate scope is bounded. Source coverage is documented and incomplete where access failed. File-link checking does not verify remote URLs or Markdown anchors. G0 can reject the mechanism, and later stages may remain infeasible on the hardware or physiology; failed gates stop advancement.
