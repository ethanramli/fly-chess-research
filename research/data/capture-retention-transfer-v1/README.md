# Capture-retention family v1 — preflight rejected

Preserved initial position-family build and protocol. Do not train on it or treat its final split as independent evidence. The first cue preflight found two duplicate projected patterns in the 16-candidate final set: one repeated failure cue and one repeated success cue. The repetition came from two boards that differed only by a nuisance pawn dropped by the 14-cell projection, so the nominal eight panels overstated independent input coverage.

The v2 family retains the training and validation files but replaces the final family with varied blocker geometry and an alternate diagonal attacker. See [v2](../capture-retention-transfer-v2/README.md). V1 remains available for exact historical reconstruction; its inputs are preserved in the [source archive](../../source-archives/capture-retention-transfer-v1/manifest.json). It produced no neural run or learning result.
