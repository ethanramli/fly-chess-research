## Data handling

Use this directory for non-sensitive documentation, codebooks, and approved shareable or de-identified materials only. Do not store names, contact details, raw interviews, health/education/financial records, passwords, access tokens, or other confidential/identifiable data here. Use institutionally approved secure storage and follow the approved protocol and retention policy.

For each permitted dataset, document its origin, license/permission, date received, version, transformations, and relationship to the protocol. Preserve an untouched original in the appropriate approved location. Do not publish or share data unless the permissions, consent, law, and ethics terms allow it.
