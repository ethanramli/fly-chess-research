# Myelination and synaptic learning: biological distinctions

2026-10-06 researcher asks whether learning can be drilled into weights and how to strengthen "mynelnation", interpreted provisionally as myelination. No biological intervention or model change is authorized or installed by this interpretation.

## Axon wrapping is distinct from the learning mechanism

[Kottmeier et al., Nature Communications 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-18291-1), DOI 10.1038/s41467-020-18291-1, accessed 2026-10-06 through indexed primary abstract/introduction/discussion. Subsequent direct-page retrieval encountered an identity-provider redirect. The study examines larval peripheral wrapping glia, which resemble non-myelinating Schwann cells, and their effects on signaling speed and precision. It does not demonstrate chess learning, a mushroom-body memory rule, or a myelination-based training intervention. Do not extrapolate its dietary rescue experiment to this simulation or adult learning.

## Learning can weaken selected synapses

[Hige et al., Neuron 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4674068/), DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2015.11.003, accessed 2026-10-06: primary full-text summary and results. Odor/dopamine-neuron pairing induces odor-specific depression at mushroom-body output synapses, with timing and compartment dependence. This supports selective synaptic efficacy changes rather than globally increasing connection magnitudes. It does not establish our exact numerical rule, mappings, or chess reasoning.

## Project implication

Training should target useful, persistent discrimination through verified experiences and explicitly modeled local memory, rather than adding an unsupported myelination factor or increasing every weight. Successful living-fly association learning makes the proposed research plausible; it does not guarantee that a fixed wiring simulation has capacity for chess concepts or that transferring arbitrary weights reproduces understanding.

The project already keeps wiring fixed and tests gain adaptation on mapped KC-to-MBON edges. Appropriate timing, feedback specificity, representation overlap, dose and persistence must be measured independently. Numerical updates and CPU-rest scheduling are not physiological durations or consolidation. Volume alone has failed the tested repetition schedule; that does not establish that every curriculum fails. The original goal requires unfamiliar/counterfactual transfer after learning, with frozen evaluation and matched controls.

No glial, myelin or conduction-speed adaptation is modeled here. These sources refine the mechanism boundary; no new source-derived fix is installed.
