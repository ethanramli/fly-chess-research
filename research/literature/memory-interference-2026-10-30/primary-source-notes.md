## Decision-bearing distinctions

Local protection and sparse coding have experimental fly support, while the exact proposed eligibility equation remains an engineering hypothesis. A slow variable is a concrete storage strategy, but selecting its biological location, writing trigger and reversibility is part of the work before implementation. Opposite plasticity is possible in specific fly preparations; it does not establish a universal rule in every KC compartment.

The strongest counterexamples to a simple cure are already in the sources: overlap also supports generalization; perturbation magnitude matters; the slow-storage theory assumes statistics/readout unlike correlated chess; and successful retrieval can differ from preserved behavioral output. The recommendation tests these alternatives through joint retention, acquisition and reversal measures.
