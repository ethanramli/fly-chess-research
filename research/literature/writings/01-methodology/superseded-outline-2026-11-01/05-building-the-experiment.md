## Purpose

Explain how experiences, feedback and evaluation are organized so results can be interpreted.

## Planned content

- Teacher-provided tasks and independently verified consequences.
- Unaided choice before feedback and the modeled teaching pathway.
- Informative feedback and matched absent, shuffled and disabled-plasticity controls.
- Disjoint training, validation and sealed test families; resource and checkpoint rules.

## Evidence boundary

The teacher supplies experiences and consequences; lasting adaptation must arise through the declared mechanism. Unknown verification yields no teaching event.
