## Purpose

## Planned content

- Definition: applying many unrelated concepts to solve an unfamiliar problem.
- Chess examples including tactics, king safety, opening principles and endgame patterns.
- Necessary transfer and causal control evidence.

## Evidence boundary

Chess transfer is narrower than cross-domain reasoning and does not establish cognition in a living fly.
