# Chapter 1: What Counts as Learning?

**Volume:** [Volume 2: The Classroom](../README.md)  
**Status:** Planning document; chapter draft not yet written.

## Purpose

Define the observable outcomes that would justify saying the model learned.

## Planned content

- Acquisition, useful choice, persistence, retention and reversal.
- Distinguish activity, changed parameters, repetition and immediate repair from learning.
- Predeclared criteria and comparison conditions.

## Evidence boundary

A changed score, repeated move or persistent parameter alone is insufficient.
