# Chapter 5: Learning Through Chess

**Volume:** [Volume 2: The Classroom](../README.md)  
**Status:** Planning document; chapter draft not yet written.

## Purpose

Test whether verified chess experience changes useful choices and whether the change persists.

## Planned content

- Bounded puzzle experiences and verified consequences.
- All-legal action choices, candidate omissions and evaluation errors.
- Unaided retry versus delayed and post-interference performance.
- Training dose, controls and resource use.

## Evidence boundary

Immediate retries are repair evidence. Search estimates and visits are not verified feedback.
