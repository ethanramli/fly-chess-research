## Writing approach

The researcher intends to adapt this work for different competitions and possible journal submissions. Each destination needs its own format and eligibility check; no acceptance or eligibility is implied.

There is no hundreds-of-pages commitment. Around 50 pages for a volume is a possible personal planning target, not a minimum or a journal requirement. Length should follow the evidence and argument. Submission versions may be shorter, with detailed methods, proofs and supporting evidence in supplements as appropriate.

Volume 4 retains the researcher's chosen title, **The Limits of Biology**. Its claims and mathematical proofs remain bounded to the stated model and assumptions.
