# Molecular stabilization lead; not selected or implemented

2026-11-01. Research continues while the fixed cubic-exit experiment runs. This later reading does not retroactively justify its equation or change its gates.

## Experimental lead

[Krüttner et al. 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26095367/), DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2015.05.037: courtship experiments support different temporal requirements for Orb2A and Orb2B, downstream of DopR1 in mushroom-body neurons. The proposed synaptic tag links acquisition to later consolidation. The tag's physical implementation remains unresolved; the paper does not distinguish a modification of Orb2A from its synaptic presence. This supports investigating a local molecular state, without flipping a released fast-edge sign. It does not identify a working visual-KC rule, the present PAM08/PAM11 pools, or numeric kinetics for this model. Global dopamine feeding and artificial stimulation are interventions, not evidence that this simulation spontaneously produces the necessary later activity.

## Computational lead and boundary

[Orb2 aggregation model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6304141/) represents free/aggregated forms of two proteins, with activity-gated accumulation and self-templating feedback. Its proposed filter is more relevant to a specific stabilization mechanism than inventing a permanently frozen weight. However, it is a proposed continuous model, not measured synaptic kinetics; it omits stochastic molecular fluctuations and detailed aggregation chemistry. Its four molecular variables cannot be identified with the current four strong/weak reservoir masses. A translation-associated increase is not automatically the bidirectional KC-to-MBON depression/restoration rule needed here. Its persistence must not be assumed to preserve reversibility.

## Before an experimental adaptation

1. Resolve the released-cell crosswalk and evidence for the relevant visual compartment. Keep courtship, olfactory and visual evidence separate.
2. Specify receptor/activity coupling, the synaptic site, clock, bounds and every persistent molecular variable. Separate source equations from introduced connections and numerical choices.
3. Define how the state changes efficacy and permits updating after a changed verified consequence. Neither immutability nor acquisition alone can satisfy the joint goal.
4. Register early-versus-late post-learning interference, informative/absent/shuffled/disabled controls, delayed retrieval, further learning, reversal and exact restoration before new data. The current fixed run continues unchanged.
