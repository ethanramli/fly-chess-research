## Decision against the observed failure

The project [diagnostic report](README.md) reports six shared-active edges among 4,654 plastic edges. Restoring those complete local state tuples rescues old item 3 but weakens item 0 reversal below its unchanged gate. This source review does not independently reproduce those numerical results. Given that result, perfect protection of the six edges would preserve the demonstrated trade-off rather than explain how a circuit avoids it.

1. **Local selectivity:** closest match to preventing unintended updates, but only conditional on damaging activity being weak/intermittent or spatially inappropriate. Manoim does not supply a discriminator between two strongly co-active learned patterns. Do not convert relative amplitude, historical importance or task identity into a claimed receptor rule.
2. **APL representation:** strongest source-backed alternative when genuinely shared representations are the problem. It acts on activity before storage and can change overlap. However, the existing whole-cell circuit cannot reproduce Amin's local geometry from an edge-count scalar. No claim of a validated visual intervention follows.
3. **Revisable consolidation:** persistence is supported; a source-backed signed synaptic coupling and conflict-selective revision rule are missing in this source set. Neither Orb2 aggregation nor Huang's fitted PPL1 model fills that gap for the active PAM channels. Do not launch an Orb2/cubic-law replacement on this evidence.

## Strongest next discriminator, without another graph run

**Produce an offline activity/reliability table from the already audited B3 and reversal observations.** For each of the six shared edges, retain its presynaptic KC identity/group and show every stimulus-aligned presynaptic rate, above-threshold membership, eligibility, DAN drive and signed teacher update. Compare the shared set with the 19 old-only-active edges and all remaining edges. Use the frozen model's existing activity criterion; publish continuous rates and all repetitions instead of selecting a new favorable cutoff. Do not use an old-association mask in the learner.

This distinguishes **weak/intermittent spillover** from **stable, strong co-activation** in the actual model, which the binary shared-active count alone cannot do. The observations are a finite deterministic development sample; repetition within them is not trial reliability measured in living KCs.

- If the six are weak/intermittent, a local-selectivity experiment is the leading conditional candidate. Before launch, specify the released KC→KC axonal contacts actually used, receptor placement supported at that subtype/compartment, and a bounded response/eligibility mechanism. Missing placement or kinetics must remain declared hypotheses, with sensitivity ranges and receptor-disabled controls; they cannot be called measured biological values. Test an informative certified-feedback arm against absent, matched-shuffled, disabled-plasticity and mechanism-disabled arms with the frozen retention/reversal criteria.
- If they are strongly and consistently co-active, **reject the claim that Manoim alone supplies the missing protection**. The next representation candidate must be anatomy-resolved APL feedback with explicit KC→APL/APL→KC contact coordinates and APL path distances, as required by Amin Eqs. 1–3, not an unspecified future circuit mapping. If those data are unavailable in the released project graph, record a concrete no-go for that implementation; no replacement graph run follows merely from a plausible paper.

These branches rank necessary evidence; neither is a new accepted memory mechanism or evidence of useful chess learning. Confirmation families remain sealed. Any next condition requires its own source-to-solution registration, timing declaration, immutable pins and independent audit before interpretation.
