## Interpretation boundary

Exact positive eligibility includes floating-point exponential tails. It must be reported separately from actual above-threshold KC activity and from gain changes larger than `1e-12`. A state restoration on historically selected edges can establish a contribution to a fixed readout; it cannot establish a biologically available selection rule, a sole cause, the failure of all alternative decoders, learned repair, biological retention time, or reasoning transfer.
