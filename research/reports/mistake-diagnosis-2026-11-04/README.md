## B. Cross-cutting root causes

1. **Pass rules did not encode the scientific claim.** Impossible margins, automatic-zero controls, pooled retention and omitted absent-arm comparisons let executable outcomes diverge from acquisition, retention or causal learning.
2. **Comparisons changed multiple variables.** Candidate scoring changed memory; shuffled arms changed selected experiences. Equal trial counts did not provide equal causal exposure.
3. **Evidence contracts stopped at hashes or summaries.** Scheduled-event semantics, certificates, local updates and full rankings were lost before independent auditing.
4. **Proxy measurements were promoted to explanations.** Anatomical overlap, nonzero rates, one-square decoding and relative distances were treated as stronger causal or biological evidence than they provide.
5. **Operational truth was duplicated.** Runner, launcher, registration, manifest and status documents disagreed about limits, permissions and terminal state.

## C. What the evidence supports—five lines

1. Knight/bishop identity on e4 is linearly readable across the 48 tested backgrounds; broad visual competence and deployed use are unproved.
2. Memory v1 fails its registered reversal test; its acquisition design cannot demonstrate learning.
3. Engineered v2 reaches 20–22/38 new pairs and fails its gates; this does not establish universal memory or representation incapacity.
4. Six shared local states causally contribute to one interference failure; the baseline theorem excludes only a fixed-response shared-baseline repair family.
5. No audited reasoning result or connectome-specific learning advantage is established; v3 remains diagnostic pending its outcome and the defects above.

## D. Questions requiring new measurement

1. With matched experiences, fair tie choices and complete certificate replay, does the graph representation outperform raw/random memory on unfamiliar positions?
2. Does adequate positive-experience coverage rescue performance, or does the unchanged nearest-neighbour reader still fail?
3. Can other readers recover the deciding relation from saved KC activity, and does broader visual testing retain it across squares, colours and piece types?
4. Can an authorized memory mechanism preserve **every** old association while reversing the target across seeds? Are the six implicated edges individually necessary?
5. Does any benefit survive matched rewired-graph and numerical-model controls, followed by genuinely untouched transfer tests?
