## Diagnosis

For a channel repeatedly paired with eligibility and reinforcement, `g-g_min` is multiplied by a number in (0,1]. Equal exposures to two initially equal opponent channels give equal gains, hence a tied stipulated readout. Repetition shrinks their available difference. At zero reinforcement the multiplier is one: omission cannot restore any gain. This is a mathematical limitation of this branch, not proof that all eligibility models, all mappings or all training frequencies fail. An unequal reversal dose might temporarily reverse a margin; it does not supply a missing recovery mechanism.

Two actual capture-retention examples were independently checked through existing verifier code and remain failure/success respectively. Their certificates are provenance smoke checks only. They were **not** used to relabel the abstract reversal trials, encode a chess input or deliver a graph teaching event.

The depression branch passes its inactive-edge specificity property but fails as a complete reversible receiver. Do not spend a full-graph lesson block on an anatomically unverified, incomplete receiver. Next required work is to specify compartment-supported bidirectional dynamics and an actual bounded sequence receiver, then preregister association/coexistence/reversal tests before chess learning. Preserve all old gamma4, reversal-gain, harmful-dose and recovery-removal evidence; no repair is claimed here.
