The same replay also has endogenous PPL103 activity: 45 left and 12 right eligible edges have positive excess KC activity and change gains, with factors about -0.002625 and -0.009588. These counts reflect recorded modeled dynamics, not measured physiological dopamine. Unequal masks are retained, not normalized after results.

Informative and punishment-only conditions both end KQK at unreviewed unsuccessful Qb3 and KRK at already-successful Rb1. This does not establish that the positive pulse has no effect: score/gain changes and choices are distinct outcomes. Sparse positive eligibility, representation overlap, net interference and incomplete coverage remain competing explanations. An isolated saved-memory replay or separate matched representation/coverage experiment is needed before assigning causality or changing the rule.
