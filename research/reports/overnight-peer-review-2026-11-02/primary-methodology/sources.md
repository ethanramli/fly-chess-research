## Tests of unfamiliar combinations

Brenden M. Lake and Marco Baroni, 2018.
[Generalization without systematicity: On the compositional skills of sequence-to-sequence recurrent networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00350).
Generalization means using learning on new examples. Composition means combining learned parts.
The reviewer read the abstract and title/author details from version 3, lines 8-24. The full paper was not read.
Search: Lake Baroni 2018 generalization systematic compositional SCAN arxiv. Access succeeded.
The paper supports separating familiar-looking new examples from unfamiliar combinations.
Its tasks are not chess. It supplies no pass limit or biological conclusion for FlyChess.

## Counting independent observations

Stanley E. Lazic, 2010.
[The problem of pseudoreplication in neuroscientific studies: is it affecting your analysis?](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20074371/).
BMC Neuroscience 11:5. DOI: 10.1186/1471-2202-11-5.
Pseudoreplication means counting related measurements as if they were independent new cases.
For example, many moves from one game are not automatically many independent games.

The reviewer checked the author, year, DOI, and abstract through PubMed/search text. The full paper was not read.
Search: Lazic 2010 pseudoreplication neuroscientific studies BMC Neuroscience. Access succeeded.
This source supports distinguishing games, repeated moves, and repeated random runs.
It does not show that the project's proposed statistical calculation is wrong.
