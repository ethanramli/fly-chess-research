# Devil's Advocate: Phase 1 scoping review

2026-11-01. Scope: the research question brief, methodology blueprint and report README as read before this review. Role: a separate native same-provider Codex reviewer. This is a critical design review, not an independent-error process, fresh biological source verification or experimental audit. No web investigation or experiment was performed.

## Verdict: REVISE before advancing

No critical issue identified. One major wording/design issue requires a small correction. The question is suitable for a scoped literature investigation once the researcher confirms it; this review does not establish that a successful or biologically mapped mechanism exists.

## Major issue: checkpoint selection and sealed confirmation

**Location:** methodology blueprint, Analysis and decision rules: “Freeze a checkpoint only if ... pass together on untouched confirmation families.”

This can make final confirmation data a checkpoint-selection input, despite the previous paragraph separating validation from sealed confirmation. A researcher could try checkpoints until one passes the supposedly final families. That would invalidate the stated confirmatory claim.

**Required correction:** select and freeze the candidate mechanism, checkpoint, decoder and numerical decision rules using development/validation only. Open the sealed confirmation families once to evaluate that fixed selection. If confirmation fails, retain the failure; any revised candidate requires a new prospectively sealed family and cannot be selected by the old confirmation outcomes. State that acceptance/promotion based on a fixed final test is distinct from choosing among checkpoints using those outcomes.

## Minor issue: define the status of illustrative timing and load

The blueprint properly defers biological clock calibration and numerical freezing. Preserve that status visibly in the final proposal. Values selected during development are proposed experimental settings, not primary-source physiological measurements. The eventual protocol must state when plasticity is disabled for test reads and when declared consolidation dynamics continue during delay; freezing all state across a delay would measure serialized immutability rather than the proposed process.

This is a specification requirement for the next phase, not a reason to reject the present scope.

## Checks and strongest objections

- **Joint capability:** acquisition, delayed retention under new learning, selective reversal and unrelated-association preservation are present. The design does not equate retention alone with success, and finite capacity is explicit.
- **Alternative causes:** true storage change, readout offset and representation/readout incompatibility remain open. The design correctly avoids treating neuron overlap or failure to recover with one decoder as proof of erasure.
- **Biological applicability:** mAChR-B, APL, Orb2 and fast/slow state are research leads with mapping, kinetics and revision gaps. No visual-KC physiology or efficacy guarantee is imported from source titles. Their priority is a starting hypothesis, not source-verified ranking.
- **Causal interpretation:** matched informative, absent, shuffled and disabled arms plus mechanism-specific ablations are planned. Component combinations are staged rather than installed together, which reduces the risk of unexplained combined improvement. Actual eligible activity and synapse overlap are required, not just selected-cell lists.
- **Historical and operational integrity:** old failed local-gated/cascade/reservoir variants remain comparators; the cubic hypothesis is described within its actual scope. The proposal explicitly authorizes no run, no final-family access, no externally optimized update and no outside-provider manuscript processing.

## Residual limitations that must carry forward

The present document is a scoping blueprint rather than an implementation-ready long-term-memory mechanism. No equation, receptor/cell mapping, biological time conversion, power calculation or numerical joint success criterion has been verified here. Existing project source notes are legitimate context but do not substitute for the approved fresh primary-source investigation. Passing this scoping review after the correction means the question and methods are coherent enough to investigate; it does not mean the memory problem has been solved.

The next legitimate decision is researcher confirmation of the question/scope. Fresh literature investigation follows that confirmation under the invoked deep-research workflow.
