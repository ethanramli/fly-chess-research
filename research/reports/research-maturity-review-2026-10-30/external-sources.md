Not a claim all alpha3 protocols behave identically. |

| [Dwork et al. 2015, adaptive data analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/1506.02629v2) | Repeated adaptive use can compromise holdout guarantees even without fitting individual labels. | Abstract/introduction-level access. Conceptual motivation for fresh confirmation, not proof this model overfit. |
| [Pinned cesp99 fly-chess source](https://github.com/cesp99/fly-chess/blob/3c83567dfb6fa71cecc1556c75f17c8bb38fd5cf/flychess/model/flybrain.py) | Closest local fly/chess implementation; distinguish trained projections/magnitudes from this project's declared local feedback learning. | Local pinned source-analysis records and source paths inspected; no live upstream checkpoint downloaded or used. This list does not establish that no earlier publication combines the intended features. It also does not make the largest cited neuroscience study a mandatory minimum experiment for a student project. Validation requirements follow the claim being made.
