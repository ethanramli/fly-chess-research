Novelty assessment: neither generic interference nor a one-dimensional infeasible interval is a new mathematical principle. The candidate contribution is a **specific reproducible model diagnosis tied to released cells/edges and an exact certificate that excludes a named repair family while retaining counterexamples to stronger claims**. A broader or biologically meaningful claim needs replicated causal localization, valid physiology, and/or a stronger nontrivial reachable-state certificate. Search coverage cannot establish priority. Successful reasoning/transfer is a separate novelty target. Unread secondary references discovered inside Cho are leads, not verified sources in this report.
