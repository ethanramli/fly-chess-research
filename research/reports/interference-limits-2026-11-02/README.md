## Actual call path and mathematical domain

`resume-eye-graph-memory-cubic.mjs` constructs a sparse eight-step `SparseTrainer`, uses the saved eye-derived rank-current codes, and supplies tonic DAN stimulation. The runner explicitly discloses the relay shortcut: this assay is not the whole natural Eyes-to-choice route. `makeReadout` subtracts the fixed naive rest and divides summed MBON05+MBON07 type means by the **candidate-specific naive response**. The diagnostic records only this summed scalar, not the full fourteen-cell endpoint vector. `compareRestScores` reproduces the same scalar score with original or current rest.

`LocalCubicReservoirMemory.observe` reads KC/DAN endpoint activities relative to rest; the eligibility trace follows `stepLocalGated`; excess/deficit drives a four-state reservoir per retained plastic edge. Gain is `.2 + .8*(s0+s1)`, in [.2,1]. `setMagnitude(edge, base*gain)` preserves the fixed edge sign. Traces and reservoir fractions are Float64; effective graph magnitudes/activity buffers are typed floating arrays. The recurrent map applies rectification, tanh and leak; graph responses depend on the gains. Therefore a fixed KC-to-score linear formula cannot be assumed across gain interventions. Memory tuple restoration includes gain, trace and all four fractions; it does not overwrite a neuron or import a learned solution.

## Proposition and certificate

Fix one stored condition, eight scalar endpoints x(j,c), strictly positive candidate-specific naive denominators n(j,c), the assigned good actions, the same scoring coefficients and one shared baseline r. No graph state or denominator may change as r varies. Define

`S(j,c;r)=.8*(1-(x(j,c)-r)/n(j,c))-.32` and `m_j(r)=(S(j,good;r)-S(j,bad;r))/1.6`.

Algebra gives `m_j(r)=a_j*r+b_j`, with `a_j=(1/n_good-1/n_bad)/2`, `b_j=(x_bad/n_bad-x_good/n_good)/2`. For the saved Rev condition, item1 has a positive slope and item3 a negative slope. Assume both correct, even allowing ties (`m>=0`). The respective exact rational roots imply `r>=L` and `r<=U` with `L>U`, a contradiction. Equivalently multiply item1 by `1/a1>0` and item3 by `-1/a3>0`: the r terms cancel and their sum is **U−L<0**, impossible if both original margins were nonnegative. This is a concrete nontrivial impossibility certificate for a one-parameter repair family applied to real distinct inputs; no synthetic opposite labels or forced collision were constructed.

[proof-certificate.json](proof-certificate.json) stores every coefficient, root and dual multiplier as an exact rational of the parsed binary64 source numbers. [verify-limits.py](verify-limits.py) cross-checks x against raw graph receipts, saved margins within 1e-12, and eight distinct sorted stimulation lists. [check-certificate.mjs](check-certificate.mjs) independently reconstructs binary64 rationals from bits with BigInt, rederives all 20 coefficient pairs, checks three negative dual certificates and two feasible witnesses, verifies pins, and rejects a corrupted-sign certificate. Proof comparisons have **zero tolerance**. [Check receipt](independent-check.json). This proves a property of the recorded numbers; no interval bound on error versus true biological or real-arithmetic graph responses has been established.

Delay and restore-shared admit strict feasible baseline witnesses. Rev, restore-oldOnly and restore-notOldActive do not. For restore-shared, the feasible witness yields four margins [.09460890,.16275728,.22636277,.16748821]; it is an analyst-selected baseline, not a deployed repair. The original/current baseline also gives all strict signs. The additional uniform-.15 feasibility diagnostic is false for every post-reversal condition; it is explicitly a new mathematical question, **not** the original per-phase gates or an amended experiment. No conclusion about all allowed gains follows.

## Novelty and research decision

Interference, bounded-synapse trade-offs and nonlinear feature separation have substantial prior art; [the source ledger](sources.md) explicitly checks it. A potential model-specific contribution is the reproducible conjunction of **root/edge-resolved state causation, exact restricted-family infeasibility and a static feasible counterexample delimiting the stronger claim**. That conjunction is new analysis in this repository; priority or publishable novelty has not been established by this focused search. Exact identification could itself be scientifically useful, separately from a future reasoning result. It does not become a general biological limit because the cells have released IDs.

An engineered bypass changes information, storage, retrieval or allowed updates, thereby leaving a theorem's assumptions. Success would delimit this fixed model's bottleneck; it neither disproves a correct restricted theorem nor repairs/refutes biology. It may still permit the researcher's reasoning test, provided unfamiliar concept combination beats matched retrieval/simple learners. Completing biological-memory repair is not made a new prerequisite for that separate route. [One proposed causal test and critical objections](objections-and-next-test.md); nothing is enacted here.
