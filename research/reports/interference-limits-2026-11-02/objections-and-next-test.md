# Critical objections and a prospective next causal test

## What could defeat an overclaim

1. **Restricted theorem:** baseline impossibility fixes the saved endpoints and naive denominators. A changed memory state, cell pooling, denominator, candidate-dependent baseline, external memory or feature map leaves that theorem. It must not be presented as a six-synapse storage limit.
2. **Causal resolution:** restoring six tuples identifies a joint contribution, not six individually necessary synapses, a minimal cut, or destroyed neurons. Trace and reservoir changes are bundled. The graph edge aggregates multiple retained contacts, and full-graph outputs were measured historically, not independently regenerated here.
3. **Static versus learned feasibility:** restore-shared permits all strict rankings but is constructed with historical labels/activity. No learner may receive that mask; it is not a reachable-state or gate-passing learning witness. The .15 criterion was for its declared reversal test; uniform .15 is only an additional mathematical diagnostic.
4. **Precision:** exact rational checking eliminates certificate-rounding ambiguity for serialized numbers. It does not bound model error, Float32 accumulation error against real arithmetic, or physiological uncertainty. The observed gap is far larger than displayed rounding, but a formal interval robustness theorem was not established.
5. **Attribution:** soma side is not receiving compartment side; current six KCs annotated right feed a left-soma-annotated MBON05. PAM08's averaged 46-cell signal cannot identify which DAN is essential. APL structural reciprocity is not an intervention result. Transmitter/behavioral valence and reader coefficient signs remain separate.
6. **Generalization and statistics:** selected seed4102/item3 is a development failure. Repetitions are neither independent animals nor anatomical replications. No confidence interval or universal biological bound follows. Existing pooled gates and absent-feedback instability remain preserved.
7. **Assay route:** saved sparse stimulation is an eye-derived rank-code relay and consequences are arbitrary assigned associations. This experiment did not teach safe capture or combine concepts, and cannot pass the researcher's reasoning definition.
8. **Literature:** local APL, axonal specificity, bounded synapses, metaplasticity and connectome cognitive tasks have prior work. Exact roots could make a model diagnosis reproducible, but do not establish priority or universal living-fly behavior.
