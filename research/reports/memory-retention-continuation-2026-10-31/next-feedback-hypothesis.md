## Falsifiable alternative

Current rules use local KC activity, eligibility and a fixed consequence pulse. They do not use the circuit's current prediction to regulate susceptibility. Hypothesis: a prediction-sensitive neural teaching pathway can protect ordinary stable learning while responding more strongly to a confidently wrong choice. It must compute that distinction from neural activity; the teacher cannot announce reversal, assign updates or teach an unchosen move.

Bennett/Philippides/Nowotny2021, DOI10.1038/s41467-021-22592-4, [primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8105414/) motivates MBON feedback to DANs and explicitly distinguishes a constrained circuit from augmented, partly proposed connections. Its unrestricted cross-valence rule cannot be installed without verifying the required paths. Iigaya2016's separate surprise system has the same unresolved implementation issue. The rejected clipped self-limiting diagnostic does not implement those neural circuits and already failed; do not relabel it as a new solution.
