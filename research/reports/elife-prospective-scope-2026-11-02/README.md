## Recommendation and distance to the next scientific step

Keep the researcher's priority: **can a simple-organism-derived system apply separately learned concepts to unfamiliar problems?** Study the computational system actually implemented; no model result alone answers whether a living fly reasons. Recommend investigating whether a frozen fly-connectome-derived representation plus disclosed engineered experience memory supports consequence-sensitive unfamiliar-position transfer better than matched retrieval and non-connectome controls. This is a focused, falsifiable step toward Volume 3. It is not yet the full reasoning claim. See [researcher definition and phased plan](../../../documentation/design/research-goals/restart-plan-2026-10-30.md) and [five-volume authorization](../../../documentation/design/research-goals/five-volume-series.md).

We have an integrated engineering pilot, before a demonstrated learned chess relation on the restart route. The [live comparison](../long-term-memory-v1-live-comparison-2026-11-01/README.md) has one development seed, four arbitrary associations, shared graph-derived sparse relay descriptors and a shortened delay. All three informative readouts finish 4/4; memory-only and hybrid each make 23/24 correct reversal choices. No incremental neural-score benefit or unfamiliar-cue transfer follows. The [passive reader closeout](../overnight-eyes-reader-repair-2026-11-01/REPORT.md) establishes a tested observation contract, not safe-capture information or a deployed chooser. The complete 48-pair eye test has not run and F04 remains unresolved.

Distance is a chain of missing evidence, not a percentage or calendar estimate: specify a cross-cue mechanism and information boundary → resolve the chosen input-test route → demonstrate own-choice learning of one chess relation with controls and retention → freeze and confirm disjoint/counterfactual transfer → test prior-learning benefit → combine separately learned concepts. Biological-memory repair is **not** a prerequisite for the engineered track. Actual implementation, development sample sizes and graph workload remain to be registered; no defensible count of hours to success exists.

## What would count as a connectome contribution?

The current “memory-only” baseline shares graph-derived cues and executes a shadow graph; it isolates use of the online neural score, not all use of the connectome. A same-representation lookup comparator and a no-connectome representation comparator answer different questions. This distinction is required by the inspected [policy](../../../.codebase/src/neural-learning/memory-backed-policy-v1.mjs) and [memory](../../../.codebase/src/neural-learning/episodic-consequence-memory.mjs), not a theoretical objection alone.

A causal topology claim needs identical learning/controller/information budgets, released-versus-constrained-null representations, adequate non-silent null dynamics, reproducible held-out advantage and targeted loss/restoration of that advantage. A fixed graph that improves similarity geometry can contribute useful inductive bias; that does not show ongoing neural concept manipulation. To attribute online decision benefit, removing neural scores while keeping graph descriptors and memory fixed must materially impair unfamiliar choices. To attribute learned memory, resetting or mismatching the relevant learned content must impair performance and restoring it must recover the effect. See protocol E4.

## Figure plan and minimum paper package

1. System and information-flow figure: separate teacher/verifier from deployed input, graph representation, external memory, policy and optional search; mark permitted persistent state and candidate-order restoration.
2. Input-route evidence: piece/marker/rights observability, diagnostic decoder by whole-game split, per-cell endpoint range and exact F04 scope. Display original blocked corner and amended measure separately.
3. E1 acquisition, further-learning retention and reversal: all seeds/items and controls, own-choice histories and all-legal choices. Familiar recall and unseen-cue results separated.
4. Transfer/counterfactual figure: paired outcome flips, distance/exact-hit strata, simple/retrieval controls and uncertainty by independent game. Only if B/C ran: prior-memory and all-versus-single-concept panels.
5. Attribution figure: raw/random/rewired representations, circuit ablation/unrelated lesion/restoration, separate graph-feature and online-score contributions. Caption explicitly limits anatomical and algorithmic replication.
6. Reproducibility/resource panel: CPU/wall/RSS, forward counts, capacity/eviction curves, audit coverage and failure/unknown flow. Biological prediction panel only when supported, not speculative arrows presented as results.

For a first eLife submission, prefer one coherent supported causal story with figures 1–3 and 5–6 plus the appropriate transfer evidence. Do not write composition results if only E1 was completed. Supporting package: dated protocol/amendment history, independent source provenance, clean source release, immutable manifests/raw receipts, figure commands, sealed-test custody record, all attempted candidates and failed controls, AI disclosure and independent analyst assessment. Human comparisons and Elo are not requirements for this narrower model claim; they need later protocols if claimed.
