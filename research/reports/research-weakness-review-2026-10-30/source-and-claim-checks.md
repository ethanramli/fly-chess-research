## 5. Analytical and raw-output cross-checks

### Inactive recovery, overlap, saturation and readout

With D=-d, a=ηdt d and k=0, ReversalMemory's unclipped update becomes **g'=(1-a)g+a**. Under the explicit stability guard 0≤a≤1, a depressed g moves toward 1 without local KC activity. This confirms an equation property, not the fraction of old forgetting it caused.

For g≤1 and k≥0, the general recurrence is **g'=(1-a)g+a(1-k)≤1**. Together with initialization g=1 and clipping below, it cannot learn g>1 under these assumptions. Alpha's negative-D active update is monotone nonincreasing. Thus the 1.8 diagnostic corner is not reached merely because the constructor accepts maxGain=1.8. A future approved rule can change this argument.

Shared-KC reactivation can still alter an eligible synapse; setting eligibility to zero on truly inactive edges does not solve interference on active shared edges. A rank reversal alone does not isolate storage erasure: both the original candidate and its competitor/readout may change. Identical full input/state requiring opposite responses is an information conflict, not proof that additional memory state is universally useless. Relevant history could change what counts as the full input.

All are mathematical/model-level distinctions. No new recurrence simulation or numerical test was executed.

### Decoder and wiring outputs

The saved decoder check has two successful solver/strong-linear self-tests and consistently poor contextual-relational decoding. Its record says 8-fold pair-grouped evaluation replaced leave-one-pair-out for runtime; the code fits standardization only on training rows. This is a sound leakage precaution.

At N=48 its null summary has one pass in 40 datasets. The observed **2.5%** is the empirical frequency in those 40 artificial datasets, **not** a precise population false-positive estimate, the full eye gate's error rate, or proof that a particular threshold/implementation is wrong. The raw comparison only checks the decoder-count threshold and does not reproduce all graph-vs-raw/usable-strength gates. Cross-validated predictions share fitted data, so an independent-binomial tail is not automatically a calibrated probability for this procedure. No fresh null analysis ran.

The wiring output's aggregate sums and script selection were inspected. It lists 480 direct MBON→DAN edges; KCg-d's largest positive output totals go to MBON05/27/09, while KCab-p strongly targets MBON07/06. The old readout's low direct fractions motivate rerouting investigation. They do not prove a cell is functionally blind, establish an optimal new valence readout, or measure the effect of the mixed MBON05→PAM08 signs. No claim that the published graph bytes were independently reverified in this turn.

### Certificates and legal task families

The producer and checker both require an initial legal capture. Both enumerate the legal reply list and define immediate recapture onto the capturing move's destination. They bind board/history/actor/action and reject unknown evidence as a teaching event.

Under [FIDE Articles 3.7.2 and 3.7.3.1–3.7.3.2](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/e012023), an en passant reply requires the preceding move to have been a two-square pawn advance; such an advance is a noncapture. That is inconsistent with the proposed initial-capture task. This establishes **F03 as a specification defect** without generating any task board. If the plan meant an en passant *initial* capture, that needs an explicit correction and appropriate certificates, not an accusation of an engine bug.
