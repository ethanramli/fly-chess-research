Keep graph, initialization, seed, readout, public encoder, delayed eligibility/plasticity constants, teaching amplitude, task and board unchanged. Use only the previously inspected training fixture; no validation/final selection. Preserve all prior protocols and archives. Compare adaptive targeting against a separate fixed-initial-error arm at the same maximum budget, in addition to absent, matched-shuffled and disabled controls. Otherwise a larger event budget would confound the targeting comparison.

Budget: four two-event blocks, eight events per arm. At each block, measure the informative learner's all-legal unaided ranking at fixed memory, choose its unique top action if independently certified failure, and teach its exact consequence; follow with the highest-ranked independently certified mate alternative (lexical UCI tie-break). If the top choice is already a mate, stop selecting new blocks. Ties or unknown verification stop without inventing an error. The block selection assessment must be accounted for and replayed in every arm at identical positions and presentation boundaries. Do not reuse an old score after intervening plasticity.

Record the entire informative experience schedule including selection rankings, certificates, alternative, condition-independent timing, and stop reason. Replay the same completed schedule in absent, shuffled and disabled arms from identical initialization. Shuffle whole consequence-channel events by a frozen permutation across the completed even-length schedule; preserve count and magnitude and assert the assignment differs. A two-event swap is an anticorrelated control and must be labeled as such, not called mixed. No event on incomplete/unknown proof. Fixed-target informative comparator receives initial Qe6 failure and the same verified positive alternative, with matching event count and assessment exposure; it isolates adaptive selection from increased dose on this historical fixture.

Persist selected schedule and cursor alongside after-memory and pending receipt atomically before acknowledgment. Resume into a new directory without reselecting an already committed event. Independent audit must reproduce informative selection from pre-feedback state, the exact schedule, certificate checks, all receipts, and baseline/final all-legal rankings for every arm. Software verification alone cannot establish this.

Primary result: the unique all-legal final choice meets the checked mate criterion; report all conditions, the fixed-target comparator, margins and regressions without changing gates. Budget stop is failure to repair within this bound, not universal incapacity. Immediate taught-board repair remains insufficient for association/coexistence/reversal, trained sentinel preservation, measured retention, disjoint/counterfactual transfer, or the 8-of-10 criterion. Outcome pulses and ordinal prediction records do not implement calibrated prediction-error coding. Change representation only in a subsequent, separately declared factor experiment if adaptive targeting fails.
