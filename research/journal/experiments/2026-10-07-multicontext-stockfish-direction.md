# Shared-memory result and Stockfish teaching direction

The shared-memory multi-context run is terminal. Its frozen protocol tested one shared memory across two related smothered-mate training positions and exact absent, shuffled and disabled controls. The independent audit passed 154 certificate checks, 96 all-legal panels, and 24 local recurrence checks. Informative learning changed both target rankings relative to controls, ending at ranks 11/32 and 5/30, but did not make either move the top choice. Both unaided final choices and both fixed-four-evaluation searches remained failures. The audited details and report hash are in the [run record](../../../archive/early-development/research/reports/shared-memory-multi-context-coverage-v1-2026-10-07/README.md).

This is evidence that informative replay affected the preference ranking in these two training contexts, but did not yet yield usable choices. Both positions are from one motif family. This does not demonstrate unfamiliar transfer or reasoning.

The researcher then authorized Stockfish evaluations as a teaching source, proposing to compare the fly's own position evaluation to an engine target and use accuracy-dependent feedback through a dopamine-like teaching pathway. This may offer a richer calibration signal than binary mate outcomes. It requires a fixed neural position-value output; the current MBON score ranks candidate moves and cannot be compared directly to centipawns. A signed error-to-teaching signal and its eligibility/credit timing also remain unspecified. Engine settings must be pinned, and Stockfish targets are an engine-conditioned reference, not ground truth.

The next preparation is to define the neural value output and target transform on paper, then test the mapping with saved activations before graph training. A future frozen comparison should include Stockfish-value feedback, consequence-only feedback, absent, shuffled and disabled-plasticity controls, with held-out position families and counterfactual transfer. Do not call the artificial error signal dopamine until its identity and dynamics are justified.
