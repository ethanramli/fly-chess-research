# 2026-10-07 cached root assessment in Monte Carlo

The preceding summary-cache result used repeated assessment panels; this follow-up tested a repeated computation in the actual all-legal Monte Carlo path. On the same trained memory and32-move root, the benchmark freshly assessed all legal actions twice, then ran two-evaluation PUCT. Uncached PUCT recomputed the root; cached PUCT reused that exact fresh summary. Both modes evaluated the child with new neural activity.

Independent certificates show both selected moves fail the mate task. The v4 protocol pins its own runner and prerequisites; independent auditor passes and all120 tests pass. Frozen-source v1-v3 mistakes are preserved and explicitly excluded. No new feedback, acquisition or transfer was measured.
