# 2026-10-31: Eyes v1 replaces the input and attack-map line

**Researcher direction.** The scientific reviews showed outdated components. The vision line (input v1-v3, attack map v1-v2) should evolve to "eyes v1", similar to Carlo Esposito's eyes, with his code usable because he is credited. Fix one small problem at a time.

**What was built.** A fixed renderer from board and candidate move to photoreceptor currents. [Design](../../../documentation/design/chess-input/eyes-v1.md), [static check](../../reports/eyes-v1-2026-10-31/README.md). The placement comes from Esposito through the pinned graph. His placement code was not ported, because its output is already stored there.

## Mistakes and pivots found while building

1. **Plan's point sampling failed on geometry (mistake in the plan, section 3.2).** The plan assumed a photoreceptor can sample a picture at its position. Each square has only 9 to 51 distinct columns, and the weighted binning leaves some squares one or two lattice rows tall. Point sampling gave identical input for some piece pairs on some squares. **Evidence:** 21 of 960 cases for the adopted pictures, and 35 to 74 of 960 for the browser piece sets at 16 x 16 ([output](../../reports/eyes-v1-2026-10-31/compare-sampling-output.txt)). **Limit:** this is static geometry. It shows missing input differences. It does not show what the graph does with them. **Change:** each photoreceptor reads the mean ink over a viewing cell. No identical case remains; the worst mean difference is 0.0527.
2. **First picture source dropped (pivot).** The first prototypes used the vendored browser piece PNGs, as the reuse instruction suggests. The repository's MIT notice covers the repository, and nothing read states where the art came from. Their set names resemble another site's themes; that is a suspicion, not a finding. **Change:** DejaVu Sans glyphs, whose licence is explicit. The worst mean difference also improved (0.0527 against 0.0207 to 0.0347).
3. **First glyph rendering was too small (mistake).** A first DejaVu render left the glyphs small in the tile (worst difference 0.022). **Change:** crop to the glyph and fit it to 87.5% of the tile (0.0527).

## Known and unresolved

- Two moves on one board share most of their input (overlap about 0.75). Telling moves apart may be hard. This is measured at the input only.
- The pathway problem from the [old eye-route report](../../../archive/early-development/research/reports/stage1-eye-route-2026-10-29/README.md) is unchanged: about 5% of cue drive in visual KCs, measured with the older rendering. No gain was added.
- Eyes v1 has not run on the full graph. **Next test:** the read-only eye test (plan section 7), after its open F03/F04 corrections are decided. Its Graph, Raw-eyes and shuffled-wiring comparators and shortcut probes are not built.
- The [plan](../../../documentation/design/research-goals/restart-plan-2026-10-30.md) said the first test may not change the rendering. Freezing it now preserves that rule.
