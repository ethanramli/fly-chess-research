# Ethics, permissions, and responsible practice

This is a reminder list, not an approval. Requirements depend on discipline, institution, location, funder, and study design. Ask a mentor or the relevant institutional office before starting any regulated activity.

- [ ] Identify whether the project involves people, animals, private or sensitive data, protected sites/material, or risks to researchers.
- [ ] Ask the relevant institution or ethics review body whether review is required, and obtain approval before recruitment, intervention, or accessing restricted data when required.
- [ ] Plan informed consent, participant safety, withdrawal, and fair recruitment where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm permissions, licenses, and terms for datasets, instruments, images, archives, and other materials.
- [ ] Decide how data will be stored, access-controlled, retained, shared, or destroyed. Keep identifiable/confidential data out of this workspace unless approved storage and safeguards are explicitly in place.
- [ ] Agree with collaborators on roles, authorship criteria/order, contributions, and conflict resolution early; revisit if roles change.
- [ ] Plan how to report funding, conflicts of interest, limitations, deviations, and negative or inconclusive findings.
- [ ] Check whether preregistration, a registered report, or a protocol repository is suitable and required.
- [ ] Keep a record of approvals, dates, protocol versions, and conditions in approved secure storage; note non-sensitive references here.
