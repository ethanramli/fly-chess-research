Contacts will be ranked on five separate dimensions rather than prestige:

1. **Scientific fit:** direct relevance to the unresolved reward, plasticity, reversal, interface, or connectomics question.
2. **Question fit:** whether the proposed bounded ask falls within the person's demonstrated current work.
3. **Reachability:** an official public professional contact route and no published instruction against unsolicited inquiries.
4. **Practicality:** timezone, career stage, and the likelihood that the person can offer a pointer without assuming supervision.
5. **Evidence quality:** current official role plus primary work that supports the personalization.

The final sequence should start with a small, mixed set rather than emailing everyone at once: one directly relevant early-career researcher, one Asia-Pacific researcher, and one senior specialist. Results from that first wave should inform later wording and targeting. Prestige alone must not determine order.

## Outreach waves

### Wave 0 — preparation

- Fill in the student's preferred name, exact school/status, grade/year, and stable public project link.
- Read the cited work closely enough to check every personalized sentence.
- Confirm whether the ask is advice, a referral, a formal program route, or possible mentorship; do not combine all four.
- Re-verify the role and contact address on the day of sending.
- Ask a teacher, counselor, or parent/guardian to review the first message because the sender is a minor or high-school student.
