## Where to start

For the current reversal problem, start with **Michael Nitabach and Johannes Felsenberg**. Their work helps distinguish circuit-generated omission signals, competing traces and changed expression. They are not interchangeable: punishment omission, extinction, reward devaluation and reward-contingency reversal use different protocols.

For the physiological mechanism and input assumptions, read **David Owald, Andrew Lin, Toshiharu Ichinose and André Fiala**. They address postsynaptic storage, local inhibition, recurrent consolidation and compartment-local measurements. **Emmanuel Périsse** is especially relevant to defining relative versus absolute feedback value.

These are project-fit priorities, not claims that anyone is available to mentor a high-school student. Li Yan McCurdy's historical fly work fits closely, but her current addiction-recovery program makes a brief question/referral request more appropriate. Preat and Plaçais jointly lead one laboratory; contacting both would not add two independent groups.
