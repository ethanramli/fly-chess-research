## Why relevant

Pisokas combines insect circuit modeling with neural-symbolic learning and robotics, and his stated current research concerns how brains support efficient learning. This offers a valuable modeling perspective on what connectome constraints do and do not determine. His work is primarily navigation/central-complex focused, not mushroom-body reward learning.
