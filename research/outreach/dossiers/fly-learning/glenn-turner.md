## Why relevant

Turner's group studies how sensory information is represented in fly neural activity and how associative learning modifies those representations. That offers a grounded comparison for this project's unverified board-to-neural encoding, sparse circuit response, and feedback-related change. [Turner Lab](https://www.janelia.org/lab/turner-lab)

## Specific overlap with this project

His lab's focus on sensory representation and experience-dependent changes is relevant to establishing whether the simulated board input and measured neural readout preserve information needed for later action. This is a distinct question from the biological validity of the project's γ4 rule; reversal acceptance remains failed and no chess transfer has been tested. ## Reasons they may not be the best contact

The group emphasizes olfactory coding, neural recording, and experimental Drosophila behavior; chess-state encoding is a substantial change of sensory domain, and the current simulation has no validated sensory interface. A team member focused on modeling or connectome-based learning may be a closer practical contact. [Turner Lab scope and team](https://www.janelia.org/lab/turner-lab)
