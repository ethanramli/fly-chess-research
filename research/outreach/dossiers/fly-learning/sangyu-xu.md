## Specific overlap with this project

An interpretable reward pathway needs more than labeling a scalar “dopamine”; it needs a supported sensory/reward interface, a mechanism, and controls. Xu's neuromodulation research is potentially relevant to that framing. Current project evidence is an engineered association, failed reversal acceptance at 3/6, and no chess transfer.
