## Why relevant

## Specific overlap with this project

The project uses experimental stimulation in an engineered gamma4 association prototype. It has not established a natural reward pathway; reversal succeeded in only 3/6 seeds, and chess transfer is untested. Zhang's octopamine-circuit and mushroom-body work could help identify what must be measured before assigning functional meaning to a neuromodulator-to-choice path.
