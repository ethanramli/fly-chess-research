## Why relevant

Hige's lab explicitly studies how synaptic plasticity is integrated by circuits to change action selection, using Drosophila physiology, imaging, genetics, and behavior. That is unusually close to the project's central unresolved step: moving from a recorded teacher reward to a justified persistent change that affects later choices. [Lab research](https://www.higelab.org/)

## Specific overlap with this project

His group's work can inform the project's biological-learning criterion: what synaptic variables change, how feedback recruits them, and how resulting circuit activity alters action. The project's gamma4 association is engineered; its six-seed reversal gate failed at 3/6, and no chess feedback pathway or transfer result is established.
