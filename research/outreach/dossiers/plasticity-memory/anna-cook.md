## Specific overlap with FlyChess

Our gamma4 prototype has mapped KC-to-MBON edges and persistent engineered efficacy, but after reversal only 3/6 seeds chose the newly rewarded cue. We do not yet have biologically justified natural reward, sensory, or action mappings. Cook’s focus on dopamine-neuron heterogeneity could help distinguish which reinforcement populations or parallel traces matter for reversal, while keeping the question about model design rather than claiming biological validation. [S1]
