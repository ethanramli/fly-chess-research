## Why this person is relevant

His group investigates Drosophila memory-directed behavior, mushroom-body reward learning, motivational state, and how dopaminergic reinforcement circuits shape memory. That work is close to the project’s unresolved question of how a feedback event could act through an identified reward pathway and produce persistent, behaviorally relevant change. [S3]

## Current and relevant work

- **2024 — “Compensatory enhancement of input maintains aversive dopaminergic reinforcement in hungry Drosophila,” Neuron.** The study reports state-dependent compensatory input to maintain aversive dopaminergic reinforcement, relevant to distinguishing reward signals from a bare scalar update. [S4]
- **2023, retracted 2026 — “Multisensory learning binds neurons into a cross-modal memory engram,” Nature.** The authors’ March 2026 retraction says voltage-imaging results were not reproducible and the original analysis pipeline could have contaminated those data; it says behavioral and connectomic data were replicated. Cite this work only with that status attached. [S5]
- **2023 — “Dopaminergic systems create reward seeking despite adverse consequences,” Nature.** This work examines how dopaminergic systems promote reward seeking when outcomes include adverse consequences. [S6]

## Specific overlap with FlyChess

The project already has a mapped gamma4/Kenyon-cell-to-MBON association prototype, but its six-seed reversal result was only 3/6 and it has no justified natural chess sensory, action, or reward interface. Waddell’s circuit-level work could help us ask how to represent reward pathway identity, internal state, and competing outcome memories without asserting that the model has learned chess. The fit is for mechanism and experiment design, not validation of our current prototype. [S3, S4]

For a sparse connectome-constrained model, which single biological feature should be added first to make reversal failure interpretable: reward-neuron recruitment, state-dependent reinforcement gain, or opposing memory traces?
