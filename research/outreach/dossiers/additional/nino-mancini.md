**FlyChess fit:** Useful for the teacher/action boundary: action consequences can interact with reinforcement circuitry, so a chess reward cannot simply be injected as an arbitrary scalar without a justified interface.

**Bounded question:** What distinction should a computational model preserve between an externally assigned punishment/reward and a feedback signal generated by the animal’s own action?

**Why not the best contact:** His research centers on motor control and aversive learning, not appetitive mushroom-body plasticity or strategic cognition.
