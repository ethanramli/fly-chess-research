## Research examined

### 1. Integration of Parallel Opposing Memories Underlies Memory Extinction

2018, Cell. [Primary work / author institutional record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6198041/).

Cue exposure without expected punishment recruits an opposing memory while an original aversive trace remains. Different MBON traces coexist; changed behavior is not evidence that the old trace was erased.

### 2. Re-exposure to reward re-evaluates related memories

2026, Current Biology; online 2025. [Primary work / author institutional record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12881916/).

Unpaired sugar re-exposure reduces related learned responses, with reward-specific and state controls. The measured reward trace in selected MBONs remains, distinguishing behavioral devaluation from disappearance of that trace.

## Reasoned fit to FlyChess

The prototype's persistent old choice could arise from trace competition or expression, rather than a simple inability to overwrite one number. These studies supply competing explanations to investigate, not a diagnosed cause of our failures.

**Bounded question:** What observation would distinguish a persisting old memory that competes with a new one from a failure to form the new association?

**Possible next diagnostic, proposed only:** Proposed diagnostic: retain separate cue-evoked neural contrasts and behavior across acquisition, reversal and a later probe; include a control that never receives informative feedback. Any new neural mechanism would need identified circuits and a frozen prospective test.

**Limits and reasons this may be the wrong contact:** Extinction, reward devaluation and contingency reversal are distinct protocols. Neither cited result supplies a gamma4 reversal rule or a natural chess reward pathway.

The current six-seed follow-up improved rewarded preference in 6/6 but reversed correctly in 3/6. Both completed acceptance protocols failed. The mechanism remains biologically unvalidated and the chess round records feedback without learning. This dossier does not adopt a new learning mechanism, change a completed gate, or infer endorsement.
