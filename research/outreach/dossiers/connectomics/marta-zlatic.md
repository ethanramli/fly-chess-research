## Why relevant

Zlatic's group studies learning, memory-based decision-making, action selection, and how circuit structure relates to function in Drosophila larvae. Her work is directly relevant to the project's need to understand how a learned value guides a selected action and how that pathway reaches motor output. The model uses the adult brain and an engineered gamma4 mechanism, so developmental and circuit differences must remain explicit.

## Current/recent work

- **2025 — “Descending neurons integrate learnt information from mushroom body with context to promote escape behaviour,” bioRxiv preprint.** The study traces a larval pathway from mushroom-body output and nociceptive context through descending neurons to a nerve-cord command neuron. This remains a preprint, not peer-reviewed evidence. [Preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.09.30.679458).
- **2025 — “A split-GAL4 driver line resource for Drosophila neuron types,” eLife.** A large resource supporting cell-type-specific experimental access, including contributors from the Zlatic group. [eLife article](https://elifesciences.org/articles/98405).
- **2023 — “The connectome of an insect brain,” Science.** A synaptic-resolution map of the larval Drosophila brain produced by the Zlatic and Cardona groups with collaborators. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.add9330).

## Specific overlap with this project

The strongest conceptual overlap is not adult gamma4 plasticity; it is the unresolved path from a learned signal to a selected action, including context and downstream motor consequences. This project has only an engineered association prototype, and its frozen reversal acceptance failed (3/6 correct choices). Its puzzle round currently records feedback without learning; adult sensory/action/reward mappings and chess transfer remain unresolved. Larval circuit findings cannot be transferred as adult mappings without evidence.

## What to ask

For a model that claims feedback-guided choice, what minimum evidence would you require to show that a learned value signal causally changes a selected action through an identified circuit path?
