# Research records

For today's conclusions, read [current research status](../documentation/current-status.md). For specifications and hypotheses, use the [documentation guide](../documentation/README.md).

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [project](project/README.md) | Overview, research framing, decisions and ethics |
| [literature](literature/README.md) | Source evidence, search history and source-to-solution register |
| [journal](journal/README.md) | Dated discussions, experiments and outreach history |
| [data](data/README.md) | Frozen protocols, manifests, streamed datasets and declared fixtures |
| [reports](reports/README.md) | Raw measurements, receipts, checkpoints and independent audits |
| [runs](runs/README.md) | Historical supervised baseline checkpoints |
| [outreach](outreach/README.md) | Expert dossiers, campaign logs and individual drafts |

Keep protocols/results/checkpoints at their existing paths; their identities may be pinned in audits. Human-facing design notes are organized by topic. Journal entries preserve failures and dated corrections rather than rewriting history. [Relocation map](../documentation/relocation-map.md) resolves older documentation paths.

[Source archives](source-archives/README.md) preserve exact pre-reorganization code.
